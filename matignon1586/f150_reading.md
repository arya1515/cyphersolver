# fr. 15572 f. 150 — Forget to Villeroy, April 1586: ciphered head, clear body

Canvas 158 left. Thirteen lines of cipher at the head (the last breaking off mid-line), then some
twenty lines in clear, closing *"…vous baise les mains … de vous donner en santé bonne et heureuse
… d'Avril 1586"* and signed **"Vostre bien humble et obeissant serviteur, Forget"**. So the leaf is
Forget's own, to "Monseigneur" — Villeroy — like f. 154.
Transcription in `f150_cipher.txt`.

**The cipher, as it reads.**

> … **pour** … **qui** … **est faire, si ce n'est que l'un et l'autre** … **vous** …
> **volontiers** … **qui ne vouldroient** … **point de compaign[ie]** … **je vous en ay voulu
> escrire … ma mémoire au vray** … **que si** … **de là où il se pourra** … **vous soyez mieux
> informé** … **le remède faict, qui est, Dieu mercy,** … **passée doucement** … **qu'il y eust
> grande [espé]rance du contraire** … **n'avoir point** … **d'avoir encore** … **ruine** … **en
> ceste armée** … **j'estime avoir aucune** … **gaigné** … **désire** … **vous confesseront** …
> **et l'un et l'aultre** …

**What it is.** A covering note in cipher over an open letter: Forget tells Villeroy he wanted to
write him his own recollection "au vray" so that he should be better informed, that the remedy is
made and — "Dieu mercy" — the thing passed off gently where there had been every expectation of the
contrary, and returns to the ruin threatening "ceste armée". It belongs with ff. 154 and 173: the
same weeks, the same army, the same correspondent.

**Coverage, honestly.** Thirteen lines, of which perhaps half comes out as continuous French. No
name or place is recovered from the cipher; the date and signature are in the clear.
