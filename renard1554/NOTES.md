# Simon Renard (London) to Prince Philip, 19 February 1554 (AGS Estado leg. 808 f. 184, DECODE R9959) — NOTES

Status: no write-up

**Verdict: closed, already in print.** Catalogue no. 202, session 2026-09-21. The letter was deciphered at the
time and the decipherment (a Spanish version) is calendared in full in English:

- Royall Tyler (ed.), *Calendar of State Papers, Spain*, vol. XII (1949), "February 1554, 16-20", pp. 100-123
  (British History Online, cal-state-papers/spain/vol12/pp100-123): **Simon Renard to Prince Philip, 19 Feb 1554,
  Simancas E. 808**, "Spanish translation" of the French original. Opens "My Lord: Since I last wrote to your
  Highness French plots have been discovered to show that Courtenay and the Lady Elizabeth, by means of
  intermediaries called Peter Carew, Wyatt, Crofts and my Lord Thomas (Grey), conspired to throw the Queen of
  England into the Tower..."; ends "The powers sent by way of France have come, and the Pope has granted a
  dispensation." About 650 words. A second, holograph clear letter of the same day (also E. 808) follows it.
- Tomokiyo (cryptiana, "spanish2C", cached at `vasto1527/prior/spanish2C_now.htm` l. 771) identifies the cipher as
  the Granvelle-Saint Mauris cipher of 1547-48 (Pich-Ponce's PP1) and reads the opening "Monsieur, ... puis mes
  dernieres lectres" (French) — the same opening as Tyler's "Since I last wrote". Tomokiyo heads it "to Charles V";
  the address leaf says "Monseigneur le prince d'Espaigne", so DECODE's recipient (Philip) is right.
