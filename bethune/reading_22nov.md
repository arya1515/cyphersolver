# 22 November 1601, Henri IV to Philippe de Béthune — BnF fr. 3484 no. 12, ff. 46r–47v

Saint-Germain, 22 Nov 1601. Four pages, no clear copy bound with the letter. Unlike f. 33 (a solid
page of cipher), this letter alternates clear French with short ciphered passages inside the same
lines, so the clear text carries most of the sense and frames every cipher span.

Transcribed from the full-resolution Gallica images (ark btv1b52523722c, canvases 99–102) cut into
line thirds at 3× with `bethune/cut.py` + `bethune/sheet.py`.

Conventions: clear text in roman, abbreviations expanded silently (`led.` = ledit, `p` = par/pour).
Cipher runs are given between « » in the glyph labels of `key.tsv`. `?` marks an uncertain glyph.
The cipher spans are transcribed at the same fidelity as the f. 33 v2 reading, i.e. about 70 % of
glyphs right (see the oracle measurement in NOTES.md §"What the ceiling is"), so the decoded
passages below are a substance, not a reading.

## What the clear text already gives

The letter answers Béthune's despatch of 29 October, received 18 November. In clear:

* the King is glad of the contentment "they" received from the assurance Béthune gave them of the
  continuation of his friendship, and of the confidence he has in theirs;
* he asks Béthune to keep up the same conduct, and says he will likewise hold back in the matter of
  the courtesies Béthune has received;
* on the main business: **"Il me semble que [20] [S 12], qui sont douez de toute prudence, ont faict
  faulte de n'avoir rompu ce coup, car ilz l'eussent faict facilement s'ilz y eussent pensé et operé
  d'heure, ainsi qu'ilz devoient. Mais ilz se sont contentez de …, meuz d'autres considerations …"**
  — a rebuke to two named persons (two open code groups) for not having broken some move in time;
* something confirmed "depuis du mesme endroit", after which the King says he must no longer expect
  to be able to …;
* at the end, the revenues of a named archbishopric: "au diocese dud. Archevesché ou il faict un
  grand proffit et pouvoir pour la reduction des deniers de l'eglise qui sont en grand nombre en
  lad. ville et aux environs, tellement que j'estime en verité que …".

## Code groups seen on this leaf

`48` (= Cardinal, known) and the open groups `2`, `6`, `8`, `12`, `20`, `28`, `44`, `66`, `67`, `69`,
`72`, `80`. `12` occurs twice (L09 "f, S 12", L15 "20 x f, S 12"), so `S 12` looks like a fixed
designation — the strongest crib on the leaf. `20` and `S 12` are the two persons "douez de toute
prudence" of L15; in Rome in November 1601 the obvious candidates are the two French cardinals,
d'Ossat and Joyeuse, who are named in clear on f. 33.

The glossed leaves ff. 49v–50r, 53r and 56r–57r (canvases 105–108, 113, 119–121, all fetched to
`bethune/full/`) carry marginal decipherments in the office hand and are the route to fixing these
groups; f. 53r's gloss is read in NOTES.md.
