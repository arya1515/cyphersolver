**Transcribed in full, 17 Sept 2026** (all four pages). Saint-Germain, 22 Nov 1601; no clear copy is bound with the letter. Unlike f. 33 (a solid
page of cipher), this letter alternates clear French with short ciphered passages inside the same
lines, so the clear text carries most of the sense and frames every cipher span.

Transcribed from the full-resolution Gallica images (ark btv1b52523722c, canvases 99–102) cut into
line thirds at 3× with `bethune/cut.py` + `bethune/sheet.py`.

Conventions: clear text in roman, abbreviations expanded silently (`led.` = ledit, `p` = par/pour).
Cipher runs are given between « » in the glyph labels of `key.tsv`. `?` marks an uncertain glyph.
The cipher spans are transcribed at the same fidelity as the f. 33 v2 reading, i.e. about 70 % of
glyphs right (see the oracle measurement in NOTES.md §"What the ceiling is"), so the decoded
passages below are a substance, not a reading.

## What the letter says

The letter answers Béthune's despatch of 29 October, received on 18 November, and is countersigned by
Villeroy ("DE NEUFVILLE"). Its clear passages carry most of the sense; the ciphered runs hide names,
numbers and the sharper judgements. In order:

* **f. 46r.** Satisfaction at the assurance Béthune gave "them" of the King's continued friendship;
  Béthune is to keep to the same conduct, and the King will likewise hold back over the courtesies
  Béthune has received. Then the core of the letter, in clear: *"Il me semble que [20] [S 12], qui
  sont douez de toute prudence, ont faict faulte de n'avoir rompu ce coup, car ilz l'eussent faict
  facilement s'ilz y eussent pensé et operé d'heure, ainsi qu'ilz devoient. Mais ilz se sont contentez
  de … meuz d'autres considerations …"* — a rebuke of two persons named only by code groups. The leaf
  ends on the revenues of an archbishopric: *"au diocese dud. Archevesché ou il faict un grand proffit
  et pouvoir pour la reduction des deniers de l'eglise qui sont en grand nombre en lad. ville et aux
  environs"*.
* **f. 46v.** Money and discipline. The King will *"donner ordre que la pension que j'ay accordée au
  s.r Camaiano sera bien payée"*, all to *"relever le party françois a Rome"*; but Béthune is not to
  *"donner des esperances ou [fermes] des promesses a d'autres"* without express order, *"afin de
  n'abuser personne ne moy le premier"*. Everything is to be done *"après toutesfois en avoir conferé
  avec le Card.al d'Ossat"*. The pair `48 17` (Cardinal Aldobrandin) stands at L23.
* **f. 47r.** The Pope's *"bon accueil … aux deux audiences quelle vous a données"*, which obliges the
  King *"de plus en plus a honorer et cherir sa bienveillance"*; an order so express *"que je seray
  obey quand je devrois recompenser et racheter la place des mains de celuy auquel elle est engagée"*;
  and *"j'en diray autant au s.r Barbarino quand il sera arrivé, lequel je traicteray le plus
  favorablement que je pourray po[ur] le respect de sa S.té"* — Maffeo Barberini, the future Urban
  VIII, then on his way to France. On the Jesuits the King refers back to an earlier letter and will
  await the Pope's answer. The leaf closes in clear: *"J'attends en bonne devotion le retour du
  courrier que je vous ay envoié pour convier sa S.té au baptesme de mon filz, lequel se faict tres
  bien nourrir et se fortifie a veue d'oeil"* — the dauphin, born 27 September 1601.
* **f. 47v.** An agent *"qui reside en Suisse, accompagné d'un certain Julio de la Torre y Travers[i]"*
  and the Spanish news; a reprimand given him by Cardinal Aldobrandin, of which the cardinals informed
  d'Ossat; Béthune is to make a fresh complaint to Aldobrandin. The King will follow the Pope's
  commandments *"avec esgalité et sans passion, puis quil y represente sa personne"*, but that will not
  stop him concluding his alliance with the leagues, *"d'autant que ceste negotiation a esté bien
  commencée et est bien avancée"*. Dated *"Escrit a St Germain en Laye le xxij.e jour de 9bre 1601"*,
  signed HENRY. A postscript, signed again, corrects the letter: *"depuis ma lettre escrite, j'ay advis
  de … en la demande que je vous commande par icelle de faire pour eux a sa S.té. Partant vous en
  userez ainsy, nonobstant le contenu de lad. lettre."*

## The cipher runs

`ct_22nov.txt` holds all 80 ciphered runs of the letter. Decoded with `decode2.py` against the key of
`key.tsv` they give fragments, not a reading — "de le resultat et sur", "leurs serviteurs", "le cardinal
aldobrandin", "attenter a este toute", "suisses", "octobre", "sainctete au". That is the ceiling, and it
is a property of the transcription, not of the decoder: see NOTES.md §"What the ceiling is", where the
oracle bound on the existing glyph reading is 71 % of letters against the decoder's 74.5 %. `73` =
*vostre* and `48 17` = *Cardinal Aldobrandin* are confirmed by their clear frames.
