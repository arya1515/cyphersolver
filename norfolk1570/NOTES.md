# Mary Queen of Scots to Norfolk, "the 20th" [1570] — BL Cotton MS Caligula C II f. 74r

Marked undeciphered (cryptiana `mary.htm`, "Mary to Norfolk, 20th, 1570?"). Tomokiyo reconstructed the
key from ff. 66/69/75 and left a partial reading as an overlay image (`img/mary_CottonCaligulaCII_f74.png`)
and in an HTML comment. This directory completes the reading from the new BL IIIF images.

## Images
- Manifest: `https://bl.digirati.io/iiif/ark:/81055/vdc_100162985756.0x000001` (from searcharchives.bl.uk
  catalog/040-001102379). Image service is level 2 but refuses outputs over ~1024 px, so `fetch.py` /
  `region.py` stitch 1024-px tiles. f. 74r = canvas image `vdc_100163185275.0x00009B`-ish (see manifest).
- `img/f74r_text.jpg` = full-res text block (x 1000-6500, y 4900-9800 of the 7144x10328 image).
- `strips.py` cuts 23 slope-following line strips (`view/Lnn*.jpg`).

Dating: after Moray's murder (Linlithgow, 23 Jan 1570, harquebus shot) — Elizabeth suspects Mary ("gives me
the wyte of Murray death"); Morton rumoured on the move; Gilbert Talbot at hand. So 20 Feb or 20 Mar 1570.

### Open points
- Unkeyed signs: ⊕ (l.2), ⊘ with inner b (l.8, a person), ø (l.13), ⊗ (l.17), {a—}/{o—} dash signs (ll.5, 11).
- l.1 "[..] your friendl[y] [b][ʃ']" and the "∠ s 3 ɹ" group after "lord" unread; l.4 end under a stain.
- ǂ standing alone = "and" (also k inside words: kan, spok, lyk, York); εʃ = "me"; б-with-bar = friend.
