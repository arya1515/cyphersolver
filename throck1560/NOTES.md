Forbes OCR excerpts (archive.org text, from `../throckmorton/sources/forbes1.txt`): `forbes1_8feb.txt`
(lines 27200-27395), `forbes1_20feb.txt` (lines 27447-27530).

So the ciphered passages were deciphered interlinearly on the originals in the State Paper Office when they
arrived, and Forbes printed both letters in full, the ciphered stretches included, in 1740. Add MS 4136 is
Forbes's working file of tracings of those passages. The cipher is Throckmorton's; his first/second ciphers
are on R9260, the third on R9262 (`../throckmorton/NOTES.md`). Which one applies to ff.100-101 cannot be tested
without images. The Feb–Mar 1560 letters on R9220 read with the third cipher, so it is the likely one.

and there is no site page. The DECODE correction (split into two letters, give their dates, cite CSP/Forbes)
is queued in `decode_updates/queue.json`.
