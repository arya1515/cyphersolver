In the inclosed Cypher (which serves only to correspond with Baron Stosch and Mr Walton who reside at Rome [Florence]) is to be perceived one in Numbers consisting of 3 Figures each as follows,

612|501|535|435|106|392|413|——

Les Adherens du Pretendant se donnent beaucoup de mouvement à Rome.

Or to puzzle it more after having made use of the Key A. in black Ink, as far as [Donnent] inclusive, which answers 371. which denotes [Changement de Clef] that the Key alters, and then
[image 1]
put the Remainder of this Sentence into the Cypher B. the Numbers in red Ink and it will stand thus.

--  be  au  co  up  de  mo  uv  em
371|436|433|342|218|446|678|417|553|
en  tà  Ro  me
453|023[?]|620|477|

The chief art of this Cypher consists in the Numbers running on without distinction till a distribution of them is made as above, by three in a parcel, by the person who deciphers, and that you make use of two Keys alternately at pleasure. [When you write you put no Stops, as in our Ordinary Cypher, but write thus, 37143643334221844667[8?]0 4175534530236208477.]

You have several Nulls to intermix as 110. 220. &c and all above 900 are so. You make use of no Numbers under 100 or above 999.
