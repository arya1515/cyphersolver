# R9492 (Soria to Charles V, Genoa, July 1523): key-A block, second reading

Source: `keyA_r9492_v2.txt`, a re-read of f.444r (13 lines) and f.445v (19 lines, L14-L32) collated with the
duplicate R9493 (f.450v-451r). The old transcription stopped at L31 and had lost words in L26-L31. This reading

Key corrections from this pass: d = m (not r). The hooked q with a long stem = R = f. 'Lo' = ç/z. Y = r(r).
P (barred p) stands for both g and a. The slanted 7 is sometimes b. cT = d, but some occurrences split as c+T = nt.

## Running text (code words resolved, [..] = doubt)

[Te] de Leon escriven a los veinte y tres del mes pasado, como lo esperavan, que vendria [aqui] muy presto el Rey
de Francia para pasar en Italia; pero hasta agora no se juntavan gente de guerra para que puedan pasar tan presto
con exercito. Podria ser que pensasen de pasar con solo la gente d'armas, con esperança que calaran los [qed], y
con la esperança de los venecianos y pensado valerse de los cinco mil infantes que tiene en Provença para poner en
el armada de mar.

E yo tengo aviso de [Bres..] [esa] como de aqui [an fec(ho)] que la [le] var los [rip] doçientas [balas] de
coseletes [..]rria tierra de los Grisones, y pienso que sean para armar a los [qed]. Y creo que los dichos [rip]
usaran con [pur] los tratos que uso Otaviano Campo[fregoso] quando se fizo la liga general, y [pasava] este Rey de
Francia en Italia, que una vez nos deçia que querria entrar en la liga y otras veçes dava palabras por dilatar,
fasta que paso el dicho Rey, y a la ora el se declaro nuestro enemigo. Y ansi pienso que haran los [rip]: que
dilataran el acordio con [pur] hasta ver lo que hara el Rey de Francia; y si vieren que tiene forma para venir o
enviar exercito en Italia, seran sus amigos y no haran acordio con [pur].

Y aviendo de ser desta manera, fuera bueno començarles luego la guerra [mul]. Desto se seguirian dos buenos
efetos: lo uno, que se desgravaria el estado de Milan del aposiento del exercito, y estaria mejor alojado en el
Bergamasco y Bresano; y lo otro, que luego seria tomado Bergamo, de donde podrian sacar dineros para alguna paga a
la infanteria. Y no seria milagro que se tomase presto Bresa o Verona, y aver tanto mas dineros para los gastos
del exercito [a ..].

## English summary

Soria reports that letters from [the ambassador] in Lyon of the 23rd of last month say the King of France was
expected to come soon to cross into Italy. So far, though, no troops have been raised to cross quickly with an
army. The King may mean to come with his gendarmes alone, counting on the [qed] coming down (probably the Swiss),
on the Venetians, and on the 5,000 infantry he has in Provence to put aboard his fleet. Soria has word that the
[rip] are moving some 200 bales of corselets through the Grisons' country to arm the [qed]. He expects the [rip]
to deal with [pur] as Ottaviano Fregoso did when the general league was made: promise, stall, and then declare
against the Emperor once the French king had crossed. So they will drag out the agreement with [pur] until they
see what France does. If France looks able to come or send an army, they will side with France and make no
agreement with [pur].

He therefore advises opening war on them at once [mul]. This would do two good things. The duchy of Milan would be
spared the billeting of the imperial army, which would be better quartered in the Bergamasco and Bresciano. Bergamo
would be taken quickly, and its money would pay part of what the infantry is owed. Brescia or Verona might fall
soon after, giving more money for the army.

The target is Venice's mainland territory (Bergamo, Brescia, Verona). So [rip] is almost certainly the Venetians
or their Signoria, and [pur] the Emperor or the imperial side in the league talks with Venice. Venice in fact
joined the Emperor on 29 July 1523.
