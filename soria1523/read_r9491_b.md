## English summary

Soria reports that someone (the [yac], maybe the Cardinal or a party in Siena) wants Siena to be governed as a
republic. He argues that decisions taken by vote of many are hard to carry out where opinions are divided, and that
it would serve the Emperor better to have one man governing Siena "de mano de su Magestad". That man would keep the
Emperor content out of self-interest, and a single man can be ordered in ways a republic cannot. He grants that
freeing the Sienese communes from their tyrants looks well, but says the city will end up under one ruler anyway,
because every Sienese wants to be master and thinks only of his own interest. He asks Gattinara to think about
securing the government of the city for the Emperor.

Confidence: medium for lines 8-19; low for lines 1-7, which have unresolved codes (yac, fob, taf, tu, pel, pug,
zib, sib) and signs whose values differ from the table.
