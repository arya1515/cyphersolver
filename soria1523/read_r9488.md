# R9488 / R9489 / R9490 (9/28 ff. 248-253), Soria to Charles V, Genoa, June 1523 — key A, reading

One letter in three copies. Cipher block f. 248r-248v (R9488), 28 lines, transcribed and collated with R9489
(full) and R9490 (spot checks) in keyA_june.txt. Decoded with the key-A alphabet (keyA.md) plus R = f, Y = r.
Code words fixed here: cap = que, lod = de, luc = duque, lih = Milan, cip = Venecia (R9489 writes cip where
R9488 spells 'uenecia'), mos = infanteria (same), lim = mil (same). Open: dus (2x), cop (1x), nra (L17).

## Reading (normalised spelling kept close to the letter; [ ] = tentative)

[... y ansi mismo] escriven de Milan como tenian nueva que era arrivado en Venecia un secretario del duque de
Milan, el qual avia traido el mandado del Infante para concluir la liga con los venecianos, y que por esto el
duque de Milan avia prometido al dicho Infante diez y ocho mil ducados, y que con esto tenian por cierto que se
concluiria la dicha liga, y por esto se partia con diligencia el protonotario Caracciolo para ir a Venecia.

Y ansi mismo escriven de Milan como aviendose [comen]çado de amotinar la infanteria de [dus], les avian enviado
una paga, y que con esto pensavan de sosegarla por agora; y para esto, y acabar de pagarles lo que se les deve,
an escrito el duque de Milan y el señor Prospero al duque de [Genova] que quiera esforçarse de ayudar con alguna
suma de dineros, y el respondio que no puede dar dineros, pero que tomara quinientos infantes de los del exercito
para que esten aqui y los pagara por un mes; y pareceles que con esto descarga de gasto al exercito y guarda
mejor esta ciudad, y quita el uso a los que tienen cargo por [dus] de pedirle dineros, y a el de darlos; de
manera que nadie atiende sino a su propio interese.

## English

They write from Milan that news had come that a secretary of the Duke of Milan had arrived in Venice with the
Infante's mandate to conclude the league with the Venetians; for this the Duke of Milan had promised the Infante
18,000 ducats, and so they took the league as certain, and the protonotary Caracciolo was leaving in haste for
Venice. They also write that the infantry having begun to mutiny, a pay had been sent them, with which they hoped
to quiet it for now; and to finish paying what is owed, the Duke of Milan and Signor Prospero [Colonna] have
written to the Doge [of Genoa] to make an effort to help with some sum of money. He answered that he cannot give
money, but will take five hundred infantry of the army to be here and pay them for a month; they think this
relieves the army of cost, guards this city better, and ends the habit of those in charge of [..] asking him for
money and his of giving it: so that no one looks to anything but his own interest.

Context: the Venetian league with the Emperor was concluded on 29 July 1523 and published 15 August (CSP Spain
vol. 2 no. 587); Marino Caracciolo was the imperial protonotary sent to Venice. 'El Infante' = Archduke Ferdinand.
