Sense: Soria expects the French king's invasion of Italy; the French hope to use the 5,000 infantry in Provence;
he fears the (Venetians?) will use with the Emperor the tricks Ottaviano Fregoso used over the general league,
temporising until the king declared himself; proposes a course from which two good effects would follow, the first
that the French army's state would be broken up; money for pay.
