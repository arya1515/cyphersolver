## Summary

An Austrian courier passed through (The Hague) the evening before, on his way from Vienna to London. D'Affry says the
minister will know better than he does what the courier carries. Colloredo, the imperial minister in London, is to send
the answer he gets there to the Duke d'Arenberg, the Austrian commander in the Netherlands. The note is a quick
intelligence report sent beside the long N.171.
