## Summary

The purpose of the Gouvernante's (Princess Anne's) journey to The Hague is not known. Some think she comes to push an
augmentation of the land forces; her (pro-Orange / anti-French) party and even the ministers are starting to raise alarm;
others say she wants a naval augmentation. If it is the land forces, d'Affry asks for orders to demand at once a
confirmation of the States-General's promises to the King and a declaration that they will camp no corps on their
frontiers; he justifies it by saying the King, trusting their word, did not order Marshal d'Estrées to secure his
communications, but would have to if the government were dragged by the English party into hostile measures.
He ends noting an attack of gout kept him from seeing people.
