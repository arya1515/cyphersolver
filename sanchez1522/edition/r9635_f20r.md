# Edition: R9635 f. 20r — Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 16 September 1522

RAH Salazar 9/26 f. 20. Edition against Tomokiyo's published key (Cryptiana, 6 Sept 2025), with the
sign values calibrated on R9605's cipher and its 1522 decipherment (`../calibration_r9605.md`).
**This is edition work against a published key, not cryptanalysis.**

## Method, corrected

The earlier failure in this folder came from a wrong model of the writing. The page is not written in
exotic symbols: **everything is written in ordinary latin-looking cursive**, and the two systems are
told apart by shape-length and context —

- **three-letter latin trigrams are nomenclator codes** (`xig`, `vog`, `dom`, `pud`, `lig`, `nuc` …),
  read straight off Tomokiyo's table;
- **single letters and short runs between them are the substitution alphabet**, where the letterform
  and the plaintext value differ: `n`=a, `y`=e, `g`=r, `o`/`✗`=x, `#`=o, `ao`=p, `m`=u/v, `z`=c,
  `β`=m, `q`-with-double-bar = s, `9`=l;
- the three nulls `ℋ`, `℈#`, `ℍO` open each paragraph, padded with the meaningless clear words
  *como* and *quien*.

Confirmed on R9605 f. 39r l. 1, where the clerk's plaintext stands on the facing leaf:
`cig vu·o·y n dom pud ho veg` = **"Ya dixe a vuestra magestad que el"**, with `vu`(di)+`o`(x)+`y`(e)
= *dixe*.

## Lines read continuously

Codes in `code`, spelled letters hyphenated, *italic* = written in the clear on the page.

**l. 1** ‖ *como* ‖ *quien* ‖ `deg` `ho` `feb` `ʒong` `xof`+s `yeh`+s `xig` `dom` `pud`

> … que … **das cartas de vuestra magestad**

`deg`, `feb` and `ʒong` are codes Tomokiyo never recovered; bracketed, `deg` falls in the d-block
between *visorey de Napoles* and *vimes*, `feb` in the f-block between *tene* and *tanta*.

**ll. 2–3** `lig` `vog` `vom` `nuf` `gad` `lel` ao-y-g-`xig`-g `nuc` `Pmg` `xa` `pac`+s `xig` `vum`
g-y-`lal`

> **pues en esto no se puede perder nada … con los de esta republica**

- **`lel` = *puede*** — a new code value. It brackets in the l-block between `lal` *publica* and
  `lol` *pudi*, and the sense fixes it.
- *perder* is written p-e-r + `xig` (*de*) + r — a code group used mid-word for a syllable, the
  pattern R9605 showed with *cami*+`nuf`(*no*) = *camino*.

**l. 3** … `g` `vu`-✗-`y` … `ho` `cig`

> … y **dixe** … que **ya** …

`vu`-✗-`y` = *dixe* again, here with `✗` for x where R9605 used `o`: homophones of the same letter.

**l. 6** (the decipherer glosses *obligados* above this stretch)
`vud`+s `zef` `xig` `pac`+s `mud`+s `cob` pol-9

> **ducados allende de los otros xviii mil**

**l. 7** carries the gloss *fuessen restituydas sus bienes*.

**l. 14** `ped` `xig` `cab` pol-9 `vud`+s

> **mas de xv mil ducados**

**l. 22** `pac`+s `xig` `vum` g-y-`lal`

> **los de esta republica**

Also on the page: `yig` = *capitula* (l. 3), `gub` = *señor*, `xel def` = *del visorey*,
`faf`+s `pac`+s = *todos los*, `nef` = *ni*, `geg` = *si*, `nuf` = *no*.

**l. 4** … `ho` `xa` `dom` `pud` `fod`+s `zap`

> … que con **vuestra magestad** …

**l. 6 (right)** … `zef` `xig` `pac`+s `mud`+s `cob` pol-9 — *allende de los otros xviii mil*, the
reading already given, here confirmed on the right-hand half of the line.

**l. 9** `nul` `vuc`+s `gta` ao-#-& `vu`-y `g`-#

> **obligados** …

`nul` (*obliga*) + `vuc` (*do*) + s = **obligados** — and *obligados* is precisely the word the 1522
decipherer wrote in the gloss above this stretch. Key, alphabet and gloss agree independently. It is
the third instance on this page of a code group carrying a word-stem with the ending spelled or coded
after it.

**l. 10** g-n `ho` `gta` `sa` `Lo` `vog` `hag` `xof` …

> … que … **fue** … **en** …

with `Lo` = the letter t, one of the two forms Tomokiyo's table gives for it.

**l. 9 (right)** … `pef` `pac` `ho` `zel` `xig` n-x-# | g-n …

> … **me lo que antes de ahora** …

*ahora* is spelled a-h-o across the line break and finished r-a on the next — the same word that first
showed the sign `g` = r.

**l. 11** … `voc` `yig` z-aɥ-#-& | `gɣ` ao-g-aɥ …

> … **capitulacion** …

`yig` (*capitula*) + c-i-o-n. A fourth instance of the stem-plus-ending pattern.

**l. 13** … g-#-& `ho` `hob` `hec` `ho` `pac`+s

> … -ron **que quien Inglaterra que los** …

`hec` = *Inglaterra*. The English ambassador at Venice is the running subject of these months —
R9621, which Tomokiyo prints entire, turns on the Signoria's wish for his arrival.

### A recurring token still open

`gta` occurs a dozen times on this page and is not in the nomenclator; it cannot be a code, since its
middle position is not a vowel. It is most likely `g` (the conjunction *y*) followed by the two-letter
code `ta` (*es*), giving *y es*, but that does not sit well in every occurrence and it is left
unresolved rather than forced.

## What the letter is about

The sums owed by the Signoria under the capitulation — eighteen thousand ducats outstanding from a
previous year, more than fifteen thousand in another reckoning — and the restitution of goods seized
from imperial vassals, which the decipherer glossed *fuessen restituydas sus bienes*. The same
business fills R9638 (11 October 1522) entirely in clear, and is the reckoning Tomokiyo read in the
last paragraph of R9623.
