# BL Sloane MS 3188, ff. 109–169: shorthand notes on John Dee's spirit actions (DECODE R8506–R8530, 25 records)

Status: closed, not a cipher (attempted 2026-09-21)

Catalogue entries 139 and 138 (138 = R8509 + R8520, the two dated leaves). Entry 139: "Unknown sender to unknown recipient, 23 ciphertexts", BL Sloane MS 3188 (DECODE R8506–R8530,
23 records, 67 pp., "Non-decrypted"; DECODE note: "Uncertain what these documents exactly are").

## What the leaves are

Sloane MS 3188 is John Dee's own record of his conferences with spirits, 22 Dec 1581 – 30 May 1583
(ff. 4r–108r; Whitby's 1982 Birmingham thesis edits it). The BL catalogue describes ff. 109r–169v as **notes in
cipher by William Shippen**, and other descriptions attribute them to Ashmole or Shippen. The DECODE records are
exactly those leaves (R8506 = f. 109, … R8530 = f. 169). They are not despatches and have no sender or recipient.

- f. 137 (R8509): four lines. "E.K. …", "Δ: … Puccius … fortiss[im]a foemina … Mich[ae]l …", "… Prage", "K. … Δ …
  1000 fl.", dated at foot "… 25. 1595 Δ". The subjects are Kelley, Dee (Δ), Francesco Pucci and Prague.
- f. 155 (R8520, one of the two leaves in catalogue entry 138): a year-by-year list of Dee's writings, with the
  years in the margin (1547–1592). Titles are in longhand, e.g. 1549 "Mercurius coelestis lib. 24", 1564 "Monas
  Hieroglyphica ad Maximilianum", 1570 "Math. praeface to Euclide", 1573 "Parallaticae commentationis praxeosque
  nucleus quidam". "M. Casaub." is cited at the head. The list follows Dee's own bibliography of his writings.

## The script is shorthand, not a cipher

The running text uses a small set of connected consonant strokes, and vowels are marked by dots, commas and tiny letters set
above or beside the stroke. Names, Latin tags, numbers and dates are in longhand. Dee is marked Δ, as in Dee's own
diaries. This is the form of the 17th-century English "character" shorthands (Shelton's *Tachygraphy* and
Rich's system, the family Pepys used), which fits Shippen (1635–1693). The strokes are phonetic and abbreviating,
so recovering a text means reading the system, not breaking a key. The exact system has not been identified here.

## Prior work

No transcription or reading of ff. 109–169 was found (web search 2026-09-21: BL catalogue, Whitby 1982, Dee
bibliographies). The 2022 HistoCrypt cipher paper on Dee concerns Sloane MS 1902, not this manuscript.

## Why it is closed

There is no hidden message to recover. These are a reader's private notes on a text that is already edited and
printed (Whitby 1982; Casaubon 1659), in a known kind of shorthand. Reading them is shorthand work: identify the
system (compare with Shelton's 1641 alphabet and Rich 1654) and read them against Casaubon's page references. It has little
cryptological interest. Next step, if anyone wants it: match the stroke inventory of f. 109r to Shelton's
*Tachygraphy* table, then read the Actio index against the Dee diary it summarises.
