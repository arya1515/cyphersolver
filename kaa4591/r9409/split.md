  Any decrypt/gloss built on v1 P1 l.19/23/25 is wrong there.
- **#/U**: in this hand most double crosses are two short stems with one bar running through both and overhanging
  on both sides (incl. every `c#`); these stay #. Five narrow upright H's with the bar only between the stems are
  recoded U: l.8 `wpqUwV`, l.10 `T?UuV`, l.12 `wVU9`, l.12 `wDU.`, l.34 `PU9`. The distinction is less sharp than in
  R9408/R9410; l.8 is the weakest.
- **d/K**: R9409's `d` is a small square/box with an ascender (ɖ-like), not the δ (R9409 codes the δ as `b`). No big
  looped S/ℓ word sign (K) on P1. The long `f` (ʃ, l.5, 9, 29) is a different, ordinary sign.
