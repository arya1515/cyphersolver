## Manuscript page 3, short middle passage

Source: Vestigia 1286, photograph `ASMO Ambasciatori Busta 1 (110).JPG`,
4320 × 3240 pixels. Crop (2300,995,4200,1225) is `img/cont2.png`;
`img/short_left.png` and `img/short_right.png` split that enlarged crop.
`img/short_context.png` includes the preceding clear prose.

The cipher begins within an otherwise clear line and continues on the next:

> gli poteriano fare [...]
> grande partito che [...] non [...]

These isolated words are C readings, not a reconstruction of the sentence.
For example, `poteriano` comprises the p-shaped triangular sign, comma/o,
t, flat-topped e, long r, w-shaped i, looped a, round n, and final o.
The next line's opening `grande partito che` is substantially darker than
its ending. The remaining signs need comparison with other occurrences.
Do not silently complete the passage from the political context.

This passage occurs after the opening four-line cipher continuation and before
the later annual-payment passage. It is a separate span in the coverage
inventory, not an extension of page 2's line 7.

## Manuscript page 4, final cipher line

Source: Vestigia 1286, photograph `(111).JPG`. `img/p4_wide.png` reproduces
the source rectangle (950,350,3350,1400), including the date, signature,
surrounding clear prose and cipher line. `img/r1102_p4_context.png` is the
closer view identified in `cipher_inventory.md`.

The currently recognizable sequence remains:

> [...] non la mandato a questo [...]

Word division and punctuation here are provisional. In particular, `la` could
require editorial separation/expansion in a normalized reading; it should not
be used to infer a grammatical construction while both end groups are unread.

Faint ordinary handwriting occurs in the space immediately above several
cipher words, below the preceding running clear line. The positional alignment
is real; possible glosses include `mandato` and `a questo`. The opening gloss
and final cipher group remain unresolved. Record this as an apparent partial
interlinear decipherment, not as a complete clear copy or a verified full line.
