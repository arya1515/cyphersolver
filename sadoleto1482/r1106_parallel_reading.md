## Four-sign word before piu

The new enlarged colour crop `img/r1106_neutral_control.png` is derived from

contrast processing. Its first line begins with a dash/upright a, an n-like

four-sign word here is now **anzi, C**. The original placeholder `a n △ i`

Reinspection of `img/clear26b_top_enhanced.png` did not securely resolve the

## Orthographic caution

The clear copy can regularize spelling. In particular, original `guera`
corresponds to clear `guerra`; the originals also contain special forms
read as doubled letters in other controls. These observations do not justify
assuming that every doubled letter is represented by two signs, or that every
single sign in a normalized doubled word is a distinct double-letter code.
Counts and a diplomatic transcription must retain the actual signs first.
