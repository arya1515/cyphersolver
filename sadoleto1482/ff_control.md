# Doubled-f control and R1102 offerta

20 September 2026. Status: in progress.

The contemporary R1101 clear witness 7a has `affinita` in the passage
`et per la affinita strectissima` (see `r1101_final_parallel.md`). In the
aligned original, a precedes a looped upright with a descending stem and
horizontal stroke; i follows it. This provides a local **ff value, C**,
from known plaintext. It does not independently validate all remaining
letters of the two-word phrase.

Source: DECODE I5655, rectangle `(2230,408,2550,487)`, enlarged fourfold in
`img/affinita_word_control.png`. Wider context `(2150,360,2940,495)` is in
`img/affinita_control.png`; the witness is `img/r1101_clear_control.png`.
These are distinct from the short-stemmed ss control and the ordinary d:
the horizontal stroke is material to this comparison. Shape labels in the
old Unicode key are insufficient to represent the distinction.

R1102 A04 U02 has the sequence **o – ff – e – r – t – a**. The initial
comma-shaped o, independently controlled ff, raised e, long r, t, and
looped-tail a are visible in `(630,2520,870,2620)` of I5657,
`img/u02_word_control.png`. Thus **offerta is promoted to C**, giving
`gli facesse offerta como ...`. This is a reconstructed-key reading checked
with a separate letter's clear witness, not a surviving clear copy of R1102.

R1102 E01 U11 also has a compatible ff form in `img/p4_end_word.png`.
However, the proposed `effecto` still requires resolving the low stroke
after ff as a separate e rather than part of its cross-stroke. Keep U11
and the M proposal; this control does not warrant silently supplying that e.

A subsequent side-by-side recheck of the three existing control crops did
not separate this low stroke more securely. U11 remains unchanged; further
enlargement of those same pixels alone is not an independent control.

The preceding whole-word retrieval search in `u02_search_audit.md` remains
a negative result for that method. The accepted offerta reading comes from
this direct sign-level control, not from its ranked candidates.
