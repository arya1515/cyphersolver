## Petróczi 2025

Márton Petróczi, “Mátyás korabeli olasz-magyar diplomáciai és kulturális
kapcsolatok Nicolo Sadoleto követjárása tükrében,” in *Tex(t)túrák.
Egy induló generáció hangja* (Szeged, 2025).

Publisher/institutional PDF: https://arts.u-szeged.hu/download.php?docID=176350

The indexed abstract identifies the compared reports as **20 and 21 June
1482**, involving Sadoleto and Domenico Giugni. These dates do not match our
four target letters. The contents advertise a source-transcription section.
Full PDF retrieval through the web tool returned a cache miss; only the indexed
abstract was inspected. Do not claim its body excludes discussion of the targets.

**Full-source follow-up:** subsequently retrieved the university PDF directly
with authorized network access. Local `textturak2025.pdf` contains 200 PDF
pages; text extraction is `textturak2025.txt`. The chapter begins on printed
p. 17 (PDF page 15). Its source appendix occupies printed pp. 28–30 (PDF
pages 26–28). Rendered pages `img/textturak_source-026.png` through `-028.png`
confirm the heading's **Vestigia 1277** reference and the closing date
**21 June 1482**. This supersedes the abstract-only access limitation above.

The appendix is a different letter from R1101/R1102/R1103/R1106. It is not
a clear witness for their cipher, even where diplomatic themes or phrases
recur. Printed p. 27 identifies Dom Francesco as Francesco d'Aragona;
the appendix itself repeatedly uses `dom Francesco` in the Neapolitan
royal context. This is independent historical corroboration for the
identification already proposed here, not additional alphabetic evidence
for R1102's title and not proof of a cipher-word expansion.

The earlier literal `dom` reading and K/X identifications predate access to
this appendix. No target plaintext or key value was promoted from it.
The appendix contains a June financial proposal; its amounts and terms must
not be substituted for the different July proposal in R1102.

1482 and 16 July did not produce an independent transcription of R1102.

independent corroboration. No cipher reading was promoted from these searches.
