### Page 4 observation

Crop (1370,810,2960,1030) from the 4320-by-3240 `(111).JPG` includes the cipher
line and surrounding clear writing. See `img/r1102_p4_context.png` and
`img/r1102_p4_cipher.png`.

The cipher contains the recognizable extract `non la mandato a questo [...]`
with the present working alphabet, but the opening and final groups still
need a reliable reading. Fainter ordinary handwriting appears above parts
of the cipher, including approximately `mandano/mandato` and `a questo`.
This may be a partial interlinear decipherment. Its extent and precise wording
have **not** been established; do not count it as a complete prior solution.

Conversely, the older unqualified claim that no plaintext is present anywhere
in R1102 is now too strong. No separate complete clear copy has been located;
possible partial interlinear plaintext on page 4 is a distinct question.
