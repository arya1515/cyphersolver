# U02 whole-group retrieval check

Status: in progress. 20 September 2026.

R1102 A04's U02 has been proposed as `offerta`, but the interior sign
needed for that reading has no independently verified value. The proposal
remains M; no new ff entry is justified.

`search_u02_r1101.py` applies the earlier K retrieval method to U02: a
quarter-resolution template corresponding to original R1102 I5657 rectangle
`(628,2524,868,2608)`, local-contrast normalization, three scales (0.8, 1,
1.2), and the same observed R1101 writing-region masks. This is candidate
retrieval, not a transcription or an exhaustive test of recurrence.

The 18 retained candidates from each source were visually inspected in
`img/u02_search_I5654.jpg` and `img/u02_search_I5655.jpg`. None establishes
the full ordered U02 sign sequence. Numerous hits are ordinary handwriting
or share only generic strokes. Highest scores were 0.4362 and 0.4623;
scores do not measure confidence in a plaintext reading.

Consequently this search supplies no independent support for `offerta` and
no reason to reject it either. Scale, spacing, pen variation, and the fixed
masks limit retrieval. Do not infer that the group is absent from R1101.
Detailed rectangles and scores are preserved in `u02_r1101_candidates.json`.
The next useful control is a securely aligned doubled-consonant word, not
another interpretation of U02 based on the surrounding offer narrative.
