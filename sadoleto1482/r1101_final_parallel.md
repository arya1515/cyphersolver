# R1101: final-page opening, partial parallel transcription

Status: in progress. Source identification and coordinates are in
`alignment_r1101.md`. This covers seven manuscript lines above the ordinary
`Bene e vero` transition, not all cipher on the final page or in the letter.

The reading below separates locally checked alphabetic words from proposals
still dependent on the clear copy. C means reconstructed-key reading supported
by the contemporary witness; it is not a blind decipherment. `[...: M]` is a
proposed restoration requiring further sign-level checking. `[unread]` leaves
the sequence open. Word divisions are editorial. These are reading units,
not an exact glyph inventory; doubled-letter spellings are not sign counts.

```text
01 unirse [ordinary] et pigliare questa impresa et non [lassasse: M]
02 [pretermittere: M] questa [opportunita: M] laquale mai piu
03 fo ne forse sera et che lo facesse per lo amore
04 et benivolentia et per la [affinita strectissima: M]
05 che l'ha [cum: M] tuta la liga et per honore et gloria
06 de sua maesta et per abassare vna volta [W01] per
07 modo che l'imparasse de stare fra li suoi fine
```

Words outside brackets are local C readings. Apostrophes in `l'ha` and
`l'imparasse` are editorial. In line 06, `vna volta` and the final `per`
are now checked locally; `vna` retains the v-shaped first sign rather than
silently normalizing it to u. W01 is the intervening unresolved group,
corresponding in position to clear-witness `Venetiani`. Its internal signs
and whether it represents a nomenclator entry remain unverified. The witness
alignment must not be presented as a recovered alphabetic spelling.
The original `per` before `abassare` is retained even
though the previously transcribed witness phrase omits it.

Witness 7a, page 3, corresponding passage (readable transcription, not a
diplomatic letter-by-letter edition):

> ... unirse et pigliare questa impresa, et non lassasse pretermittere
> questa opportunita laquale mai piu fo, ne forse sera, et che lo facesse
> per lo amore et benivolentia, et per la affinita strectissima che l'ha
> cum tuta la liga, et per honore et gloria de sua Maesta, et abassare
> una volta Venetiani per modo che l'imparasse de stare fra li suoi fine.
