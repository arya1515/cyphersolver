# Stemless triangle: R1101 control for R1106 anzi

20 September 2026. Status: in progress.

R1101 manuscript page 2 has a cipher passage about the queen, aligned with
the lower portion of the first page of contemporary clear copy 7a. The
witness reads `Regina sta cruzata d'altro in modo ...`. In the corresponding
cipher word, a **stemless triangle** occurs between u and the a/t/a ending.
It therefore supplies **z, C**, independently of the proposed R1106 anzi.
This is a known-plaintext control, not a recovered primary cipher key.

**Second control:** the same R1101 passage also has `senza`, s/e/n/triangle/a,
aligned with 7a's `senza`. Original rectangle `(600,905,970,1000)` is enlarged
in `img/r1101_senza_control.png`. See `r1101_queen_parallel.md`. This confirms
z independently of the exact original opening/spelling of the cruzata word.

The larger contexts establish the queen passage and its following discussion,
rather than matching an isolated triangle to a guessed word. The spelling
`cruzata` is retained as seen in the clear witness; the full original word's
opening is not made into a new accepted diplomatic transcription here.
The triangle's position before the a/t/a ending is the local control.
The old image filenames containing candidate record the testing stage.

R1106 page 3's previously preserved `a n triangle i` has the same stemless
triangle. With z controlled independently, the sequence now reads **anzi, C**:
`anzi piu tosto monstrarse [...]`. See `img/r1106_neutral_control.png`
and its source coordinates in `r1106_parallel_reading.md`. The faint word
in clear witness 26b is not used as the evidence for z. The following word
after monstrarse remains unresolved.

Do not apply this value mechanically to every triangular mark: the existing
p sign has a conspicuous descending stem, q has a different looped form,
and a triangle within an unsegmented code group has not thereby been decoded.
