# R1102 (Sadoleto to Ercole, Pozsony 16 Jul 1482), p. 2 cipher block (7 lines): reading by glyph

**Coverage correction, 20 Sep 2026:** this file records only the bottom-left block,
not all the letter's cipher. The facing page continues with `ducati` and contains
further cipher passages. See `review_20260920.md` for the additional readings,
the provisional `dom Francesco` title, source coordinates, and remaining gaps.

**Subsequent verification:** `verified_groups.md` resolves the title to literal
`dom` and verifies X's referent as the Signoria of Venice from R1106/26b.
It also corrects `figlio` to `figliolo` and `loco` to `loro`, and supports
`de l'armata`. The transcription below is the preserved 19 September reading;
use these corrections when citing it.

Strips: `seg/s/r1102_L0n_k.png` (numbered boxes). Glyph values in `key_working.md`. [..] = uncertain.

L1: essendo in Buda per partire per venire a Posonia
L2: sapi per gran secreto da [494] Francesco como [X = 3ί2ии] haveva
L3: scripto ad Francesco Quirino che è qua, fo[sse?] a … [Δuſ)ʒρμ иρ = ?] …
L4: gli fa[cesse?] … como [3ί2ии] voleva havere seco bona
    (…│ |943 · 3ί2ии · ƔηπΓиʒρ · 2ʒρƔьΓь · 7ь|3 · >ηбʒρ)
L5: a[mic]i[t]ia(?) et voleva da[r]gli Vegla, et voleva lo fi-
L6: -gli[o] (e bastardo) per loco capitaneo de la [ar]ma(?)
L7: per mare, et gli voleva dare ogni anno cento milia.

Gist: Sadoleto learnt in great secret (from one Francesco), while leaving Buda for Pozsony, what was written to
Francesco Quirino, who is at court: [the Venetians?] offered King Matthias Veglia (Krk, taken from Count Giovanni
Frankopan by Venice in 1480), to make his natural son ("e bastardo" = János Corvin) captain of the fleet, and to
pay him 100,000 [ducats] a year. Syntax: "como [X] haveva scripto ad Francesco Quirino … come [X] voleva havere seco
bona amicitia et voleva dargli Vegla …": X is the Venetian side (the Signoria?), a nomenclator group, since it
does not spell out in the letter key. The group 3ί2ии (subject of "voleva havere seco bona amicitia") after "como" twice is probably a nomenclator word (Venetiani?).

Anchors: Buda, Posonia, Francesco (8Γ┤б|ь7|η), Quirino (Δuωſωб3), Vegla (Ɣь→πʒρ), capitaneo (|ʒρΔωμʒρбьη),
mare, ogni anno, cento milia (13a has the same sum: "per cento milia ducati ogni anno pigliaria la impresa").
