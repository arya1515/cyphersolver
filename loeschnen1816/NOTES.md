# W. Loeschner, Vienna, 1 July 1816: memorandum on the Staatskanzlei cipher instruction (DECODE R2227)

Status: closed, not a cipher (explained 2026-09-21)

Catalogue entry: "W. Loeschnen? to unknown recipient, 1 Jul 1816". ÖStA HHStA, Staatskanzlei Interiora,
Chiffrenschlüssel, Kt. 20, alt. Fasc. ad 27, ff. 30–40. DECODE R2227 is marked "Non-decrypted", 20 pp., numerical, with the note
"Letter with short encrypted parts". The images are not public domain. They were fetched with the DECODE cookie, 11 openings,
and are git-ignored in `img/`.

## What the document is

It is not a ciphered letter. It is an 11-opening memorandum in clear German Kurrent, headed

> Zusätze und Abänderungen, welche in der Instruktion zum richtigen und zweckmäßigen Gebrauch der in der
> offiziellen Korrespondenz zwischen den k.k. Missionen und der geheimen Hof- und Staatskanzlei üblichen
> Ziffernschlüssel gemacht werden dürften.

It is dated and signed at the foot of the last page: "Wien am 1ten Julius 1816. W. Loeschner", so the surname is **Loeschner**, not
"Loeschnen". The memorandum goes through a printed or fair-copy *Instruction* for the use of the ministry's cipher keys, page by page,
column by column and line by line ("Seite 2, Spalte 2, Zeile 3 von Unten …", "Seite 9 …", "Seite 23, nach der Zahl 1918 …", "Seite 32 …",
"Beilage: Ueber den Gebrauch der Doppel- (Supplementar-) Zahlen"). Its topics:
- the misleading term "Ziffernkabinett";
- entering words in the Déchiffranten as well as the Chiffranten (it mentions a Kaunitz despatch that added supplements 16–17);
- the 3-to-6000 number range;
- Ergänzungszahlen (supplementary figures) and how to use doubled figures;
- the envelope (Umschlag) of a ciphered despatch;
- the Parenthesis and Claudatur signs.

The only figures are worked examples, and each has its plaintext written above it. On opening 9, for example:

    le Roi d'Espagne ⊖ vient ⊖ de nommer ⊖ (Parenthesis) M. le ⊖ Duc de ⊖ San Car ⊖ los (Claudatur) son Amb.r &c
    388 1949 600 477 2395 1800 983 1440 692 1369 267 2506 1801 1949 335 1954 709 1212 958 477 1211 307 2415 …

Opening 5 has a small table of the same kind (A = 1…, B = 122, … = 1182, … = 1507), and opening 3 syllable examples
("1 Com-pa-ra-tion …"). Every number group in the file has its reading next to it. Nothing is hidden, and there is nothing to decipher.

## Result

Explained: this is a clear memorandum about Austrian cipher practice, and its enciphered examples come with their readings. No key is
needed. DECODE should retitle it as W. Loeschner's memorandum, change the type to key/instruction material with status N/A (not a
ciphertext), and correct the name. No reading of it was found in print.
