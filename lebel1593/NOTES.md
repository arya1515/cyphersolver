# Lebel, Savoyard ambassador (Paris), to Charles Emmanuel I of Savoy, 1593

Status: no write-up

Catalogue entry 18. BnF fr. 3983 (Gallica ark btv1b9059406b) nos. 11, 62, 100: letters of 17 January 1593
(f. 26r-v, canvases 49-50), 10 March 1593 (f. 130r-v) and 27 March 1593 (ff. 194-195).

## Result: already read (Tomokiyo 2017)

Satoshi Tomokiyo, "Reading Undeciphered Letters to the Duke of Savoy (1593)", cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/savoy.htm
(first posted 21 June 2017, last modified 3 December 2023). He rebuilt the cipher from the siblings that were
deciphered at the time: the 13 March letter (f. 166, deciphered on a separate sheet, which gave the break through
"pardessa x partira"), 24 July (fr. 3984 f. 182, interlinear) and 5 August (fr. 3985 f. 23, interlinear). The system
is a letter cipher with homophones, nulls and a numbered code list (groups up to about 300 and above). His page
shows all three letters in this entry, f. 26, f. 130 and f. 194-195, with his decipherment overlaid in pink over
the ciphertext. A copy of his images is in `src/` (git-ignored).

The catalogue's note, "No decipherment noted", was wrong. Tomokiyo's League page named Lebel, and his Savoy page
has the readings.

## What this session did

Before finding the overlays, this session deciphered no. 11 (f. 26) with Tomokiyo's reconstructed key table
(savoy.png): `reading11.md`, a running text that interleaves the clear passages with the deciphered ones.
Tomokiyo's overlay reads some passages better (for example "la regence de l'estat", where the reading here has
"la regaigner"). Nos. 62 and 100 were not re-read. Nothing is added to Tomokiyo's reading, so there is no write-up.

Catalogue entry 18 removed on 21 Sept 2026. DECODE has no record of these letters: the full record list (10,106
records, `catalogue_harvest/decode/list.json`, 18 Sept 2026) has no BnF fr. 3983/3984/3985 holding and no Lebel,
so there is nothing to queue.
Tomokiyo also lists three more letters in the same cipher in BnF esp. 336 ff. 158-160 (nos. 79-81).
