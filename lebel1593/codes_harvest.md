# Code harvest from the contemporary decipherments

## fr. 3983 no. 80 (13 Mar 1593)
- Cipher (duplicate) f.166r = canvas 293 (w2000), f.166v = canvas 294 (w2400); decipherment sheet f.167 = canvas 295 (w2000).
- Aligned by reading the cipher letters with the Tomokiyo key word by word against the decipherment; codes
  fixed by the surrounding plain words (all C). Clear passages in the duplicate (opening, "J'ay aussy veu le seig.
  Jo Batista Tasis", "Le dict seig. duc & les aultres ministres de sa Ma.") anchor the alignment.
- 94 is rendered three ways by the decipherer (apres / negotiation / menees): a word like "pratique(s)".
- 229 is left as a number in the decipherment (it is given "a Madame de Bouillon").

## fr. 3984 no. 86 (24 July 1593)
