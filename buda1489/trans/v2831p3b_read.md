## Notes on method and on the crops
- The stacked crops img/crops/stack2831p3_L*.png are NOT all aligned to the line start: from L18 on
  they begin several signs to the right of the true beginning of the line, and their vertical placement
  drifts. Lines 18-28 above were read from the page image img/v2831_3.jpg directly. Useful mapping for
  that page (3072x4096): the centre of line n sits at y = 700 + 2.5*(60 + (n-1)*26.5625), and the text
  runs from x = 985 to about x = 2650, sloping down ~25 px from left to right.
- New / confirmed sign values found in this stretch:
  - n followed by p-with-stroke (the "n-p" ligature) = ff: "affretare" (L18), "effecto" (L21).
    This confirms the guess made at p.1 L08 ("affirmando").
  - 8 (the looped-g form) = n as well as g: "intendere" (L20) has 8 = n; "8 d n" = gli elsewhere.
  - the angular "3-with-a-tail" is z = r, distinct from the rounded 3 = o: "trovarsi" (L18),
    "presto" (L16, L18).
  - d/looped-b + o-bar = st, confirmed again: presto, questo, stato, restara.
  - L: = te, L^a = ta, b̶ = ca, b̶· = ce, b^a = na, b^o = no, b^e = ne, m^u = so, m^o = si/se,
    q^a = ra, q^e = re, q^s = ro, x̄ / x̸ / r̸ = et, ÷ w - = nulls.
  - "q^s x̄" (ro + et) occurs three times (L17 twice, L19), twice straight after "ad" - it looks like a
    fixed group or a nomenclator rather than plain ro+et.
  - "c n b" (p-i-co) recurs (L17 here, p.2 L14) and is still unexplained.
