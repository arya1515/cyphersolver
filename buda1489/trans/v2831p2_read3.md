## Note on the line numbering in the bottom third
Counted on the plate itself (img/v2831_p2r.jpg, offset 900/870), p.2 has FOUR physical rows below the row that
ends "da prestare fede": they begin at page y ≈ 1923 ("r x 8 ε a 3 …"), 1995 ("7 3 z 8 s // …"),
2064 ("c r n+4 r ε n …", the row that carries the clear "Fidem") and 2123 ("9 r x // b̶: s ε …", the row that
ends "sopra la dote"). seg/v2831p2_glyphs.json lists five lines there (28-32) and read2 numbers six (27-32):
both split single rows. Anyone continuing this page should read the bottom third off the plate, not off
seg/strips2 — in that region the strip boxes for one line also pick up glyphs of its neighbour (the L24 strip's
boxes 16 and up are L23's glyphs).
