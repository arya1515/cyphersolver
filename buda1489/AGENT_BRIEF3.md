# Brief v3: reading Maffeo da Treviglio's cipher from raw crops (supersedes v2's sign notes where they differ)

Read AGENT_BRIEF2.md, GLYPHS_1.png and GLYPHS_2.png first, then apply these corrections, all established
from lines whose plaintext is known:
- epsilon/round "e"-shaped sign = o ("honore", "suo"); never e.
- the "7"-shaped long stroke = f ("Fedrico", "fosse", "satisfare").
- "n" followed by a crossed "4" (one sign) = ss ("essere", "fosse", "potesse"); the "n+" ligature in "Vienna" = nn.
- q with superscript o = re ("riuscire", "ricevere"); q with superscript e = re as well; q^a = ra; q^s = ro.
- "8" = g ("voglia", "gli"); "d" with a colon = fi ("iustificare", "figliolo").
- plain "q" = e in most places, but = ga in "legato" (Somogyi a3).
- o with a horizontal bar = t; in "ambasiatore" and "Hungaria"-type words it can stand where b is expected; check.
- "M" with a colon = Ser.mo Re de Hungaria; a barred or underlined M = a nomenclator name (la sua Maesta = the
  Queen Beatrice? / Imperatore?) — report which by context.
- Arabic figures (8, 15, 20, 25) are numerals, written as such.
- round-e form (like c with a loop) = p; "E"-form = r; small s = c in "che" (s 9 //); straight d = s; looped d = l.
Context: Buda, Nov 1489 – Jan 1490. King Matthias Corvinus, Queen Beatrice of Aragon, Emperor Frederick III
("Fedrico"), Maximilian, the Diet (dieta) and the peace (pratica de pace), the Neapolitan ambassador,
John Corvinus (figliolo), Ludovico Sforza ("la Ex.tia vostra", "Ser.mo S."), Bianca Maria Sforza (madonna Bianca).
