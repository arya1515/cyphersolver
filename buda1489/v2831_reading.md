# Vestigia 2831 — Maffeo da Treviglio to Ludovico Sforza, Buda 22 November 1489 (ASMi Sforzesco 650, 1489 no. 8)

Four pages, all cipher after the clear address "La Ex.tia vra" (explicit in clear "…in lassignatione facta ad la
cancellaria"). No contemporary decipherment is known (Somogyi 2016: "reperibile solo nella forma cifrata"); she
printed lines 1–2 only. Images: Vestigia originals (3240×4320), `img/v2831_*_orig.jpg`, rotated −1.7°.

**Sense:** the King (Matthias Corvinus) wants peace, but not so much that he will accept terms to his dishonour;
he will not go to the Diet unless the peace is assured, lest he be blamed for breaking the negotiation, and it seems
hard to go and come away with nothing but scorn; so he wants letters from […] affirming that [the Emperor] is
disposed to peace, and with that he is content to go even if no conclusion comes of it, since he can always
justify himself. The new Neapolitan ambassador is to negotiate the reconciliation of [the Queen?] with the
Emperor, to bend it to [the King's?] wish for the benefit of his son (John Corvinus), and has again raised the
match with the daughter of don […].

New sign values from this page: 7 = f, n + crossed 4 = ss, q^o = re, b^a = na, d: = fi, 8 = g in "gli/voglia".

**Sense:** the Neapolitan ambassador was to be sent to Naples; told that was impossible since the Milan match
was already cooked, he solemnly insisted the Milanese marriage alliance was already concluded. Courtiers,
hearing that messer Francesco was to go to Naples, told him "you are going to undo what you have cooked".
The ambassador has complained in his king's name of Ludovico Sforza. Maffeo writes plainly what he feels and
leaves the judgement to Ludovico, "who is most wise": the Queen wants […], and no effort should be spared to
turn the King from his purpose; in this he will not yield "to the insatiable wrath of Juno" (Beatrice).

**New signs:** a+ (a with a cross) = Ex.tia Vostra; n^e = ll ("quello", "quella"); a word or two of clear Latin
appears inside the cipher (l. 16 "etiam apud").

## Page 4: two cipher lines, then clear text (`trans/v2831p4_read.md`)

1. …quello che sapia et possi [fare]. *(clear Latin)* Sed hoc non fallet divinam sapientiam
2. de la. Ma se e dicto ancora che la [comandata?] … non […] iuncho[?] **al Soldano**.

Then, **in the clear**, a private postscript in Maffeo's own voice:

> Credo che la Ex.tia V.ra havera facto dar quelli ducento ad m. Fran.co; che, se cosi e, la ringratio infinite
> volte de la clementia et bonta sua infinita. Et perche quelli denari non mi portavano fin al fine de decembre,
> et partendosi lui non saperia da chi imprum[ut]are uno quatrino se non dal Thexaurero Regio, che seria con
> graveza de la persona vostra propria, che li sara proveder per tempo. La Ex.tia V.ra se potera informar da m.
> Fran.co et anche da Erasmo como sto qua. Se de questa spesa non gli ne faria honor, sonno contento che non me
> ne proveda; io gli posso affirmar etiam cum iuramento che, doppoi che sonno qua, ultra quello ho havuto et
> tolto in nome del Ill.mo S. V.ro, ho speso di mei piu de centocinquanta ducati tra mi, veste et cavalli, et
> tra[?] che la provisione che me e ordinata non mi basta, dovendo tener questo loco secundo la dignita sua.
> Et perche in questa novita facta de Cancelleri forse poteria esser dome[n]ticato, La supplico ordini ad chi li
> par che provedi sia compreso in el numero de li altri Can[cellieri] et in lassignatione facta ad la Cancellaria.
> A V.ra Ex. de continuo me recomando. **Bude xxij Novembris 1489.**
> E[iusdem] Ill.me D[ominationis] V[ostre] servus, **Magister Trivilien[sis]**.

## Status of the reading

Pages 1 and 2 are read almost throughout; page 3 is read in its first half and in patches after line 19 (the ink
is lighter and the plate's resolution runs out); page 4's two cipher lines are read and its clear part is fully
transcribed. Signs still unidentified: the recurring pair q^s + x̄ (three times on p. 3, twice right after "ad",
in a slot that wants a place name — probably a nomenclator entry), the "♀" sign, a capital-E sign, a wavy null,
and a few groups noted line by line in the `trans/` files.

**Caution for future passes:** the line segmentation in `seg/v2831p2_glyphs.json` and `p3_glyphs.json` assigns
some glyphs to the wrong row in the lower half of each page, and the stacked crops drift from line 18 onward.
Read from the plates `img/v2831_p2r.jpg` / `v2831_p3r.jpg` with per-row crops instead.
