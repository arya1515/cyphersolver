# Kurz von Senftenau letters: content summaries

Written 19 Sept 2026 from the machine decipherments in this folder (read/<record>.txt, key.json). Coherence estimates are
by eye. Residual errors: transcription slips, a few unassigned signs, and the α sign, which is m in most places but reads
"Salv-" before l ("den Salvius", "Salvius per forza", "salvus conductus"): probably two signs merged in transcription.

## R3812 — Hamburg, 3 Feb 1639 (DECODE says 1638; wrong)

Year 1639: it opens "Mein jüngstes sub dato 27. January" (R3813), answers a letter of 12 January, and the clear postscript
reads "Hamburg 3 feb. 1639"; Banér is at Halberstadt and Lauenburg and Arnim, escaped from Swedish captivity late in 1638,
is in Hamburg. Kurz excuses himself for illness, then discusses the quartering of six imperial regiments and the Lower Saxon
Kreistag: Banér has broken into the Stift Halberstadt and his plundering will ruin it; Duke Georg of Lüneburg will not
resist and the Circle wants neutrality; Kurz insists the Circle be held to its duty and the regiments join the Saxons. On
peace he sees no real Swedish or French wish for it: Sweden wants half of Pomerania and an amnesty and will not come to a
congress while it does not feel the war at home; there are "grosse disgusti" between Denmark and Sweden; the Danish
Reichsrat urges the King to wait; Gallas's army suffers "mala direzione". Postscript: Arnim's visit, his protestations of
loyalty, Banér's army "zehentausend Mann, ein ermattetes, armseliges, hungeriges Volk"; Kurz advises a protectorium for
Arnim. About 75-80% of the cipher reads coherently.

- *des Banners Raub … das ganze Stift zu Grund gehen* — Banér's plundering will ruin the whole Stift.
- *Dennemarck und Schweden seind grosse disgusti* — great ill-feeling between Denmark and Sweden.
- *Arnheim ist gestern abent bei mir gewesen, communicat ad longum* — Arnim was with me last night and talked at length.

## R3813 — Hamburg, 27 Jan 1639

A twenty-page memorandum. Count Penz is lying low in Hamburg; the English king's mediation has come to nothing; the enemy
advances unopposed towards Salzwedel; only Meyer's regiment has arrived; Duke Georg lets the Swedes dominate him. Kurz
argues that a Kreistag broken up by the enemy is better than one concluded with express neutrality; fears Banér will turn
to Meissen or Bohemia; advises a defensive strategy to keep Austria, Bohemia and Silesia and not risk the army; discusses
funding of quarters and recruits. On peace he distrusts Salvius ("voller gifft contra Domum Austriacam"), weighs working
through Brandenburg or through the Danish king to sound the Swedish Reichsrat on a secret particular treaty, and calls the
universal treaties and safe-conducts "ein teufflisch inventum et ad exclusionem omnium tractatuum". France wants Lorraine,
Alsace and even the imperial crown; Sweden would be easier to satisfy. About 65-70% reads coherently.

- *der Feind gehet ganz unverwehrt hieher, zieht sich gegen Waltzwedel* — the enemy advances unhindered towards Salzwedel.
- *besser ein ungeschlossener, durch den Feind zertrennter Craistag als ein geschlossener mit einer expressen Neutralitet.*
- *[Frankreich will] das Kaiserthumb haben; mit Schweden ist umb ein leichters noch zu accordiren.*

## R3811 — Hamburg, 17 Jan 1639 (the letter with the interlinear decipherment; the one Mírka read)

Nothing can be done with Duke Georg; the enemy crossed without resistance. Recruits go to Austria; money is short. France,
Sweden and Holland mean to send an impressive embassy to the King of Denmark to prevent a congress in Sweden; Duke August
of Saxe-Lauenburg is said to be placing himself under Danish protection. Arnim, suspected over the Friedland (Wallenstein)
affair, says he plotted nothing against the Peace of Prague; Kurz advises a protectorium "in optima forma". The King of
Denmark "als ein Herzog von Holstein und ein Reichsfürst" cannot look on idly. About 65% coherent. This matches Mírka's
2012 Czech summary of the same letter point for point.

- *dass Frankreich, Schweden und Holland im Werck sein, zum König eine ansehnliche Legation zu schicken.*
- *Herzog Augustus von Sachsen-Lauenburg wolle sich in dennische Protection begeben.*

## R3814 — undated, 1639 ("Hochedler Herr")

The Swedish agent Mithoff and the Danish mediation; letters brought from Leipzig and opened; safe-conducts sent; whether
Sweden would restore anything; Lorraine, Mantua and places in French hands; Pomerania or part of it in pledge; Rákóczi
said to take French money with the Turk's consent. Kurz defends his secret particular dealing with Mithoff: a universal
treaty means war "bis an jüngsten Tag"; a secret post route from the hereditary lands via Ehrenberg and Wolfenbüttel.
About 55-60% coherent, the weakest letter.

- *Ragozi solle mit consens des Türckhen gelt von Franckreich empfangen.*
- *[nicht] ad pacem, wohl zu einem Krieg bis an jüngsten Tag.*

## R3815 — Hamburg, 6 Feb 1639, to Ferdinand III

The King of Denmark is "ganz perplex", bound to aim at peace and perhaps stalling to avoid a coniunctio with the Emperor;
the Danish Reichsrat is "irresolut und pusillanimes"; no answer yet from Vienna on the Holstein complaint. With 8-9,000
men the princes could deal with a starved, exhausted enemy; Salis sent to Hildesheim; Duke Georg, the Kreis resolution,
Hatzfeldt. See SUMMARY_R3815.md. About 65-70% coherent.

## R4625 — undated (late Jan / 10 Feb 1639 by the endorsement), to Trauttmansdorff

Memorandum on keeping the Lower Saxon Circle divided and holding Halberstadt, Magdeburg, Hildesheim and Wolfenbüttel;
upkeep "Kopf für Kopf"; Danish 8,000 men in Holstein; three grades of Circle war (defence, constitutions, neutrality,
"tertius gaudet"); the imperial army "gar ruinirt, et venter caret auribus". See SUMMARY_R4625.md. About 60% coherent.

## R4645 — Hamburg, 13 Feb 1639 (with clear copies of two Banér letters of 29 Jan 1639)

Danish interpositio and a possible coniunctio with the Emperor: the King's distinctiones proved "mere lies"; "wir wollen
uns dannoch und auch allein wehren"; will Oxenstierna again push the prorogatio foederis through in Sweden? Count Pentz:
the King must drop the mediation and denounce Sweden, "ergo ruptura tractatuum hic est principium coniunctionis".
About 80% coherent, the cleanest.

## R4734 — 8 Apr 1639, copy (to "E. Excell.", Schwarzenberg per DECODE)

The Danish mediation has expired: the King "hab gebundene Hände" and will do nothing per force; the Lower Saxon Circle
must contribute to war or to compensation; Stralsund or other lands might go to Brandenburg. The endorsement's addressee
needs checking against the image. About 60-65% coherent.

## R4736 — Glückstadt, 14 Apr 1639

At the Danish court: "Von hinnen wollt man mich nit gern dimittiren"; the Danes complain that war in Austria is bought
with peace in Lower Saxony while the Swedes stay in Mecklenburg and Pomerania, "für solche Mauren geben wir unser
Schweiss und Bluet"; a courtier "ist der gescheideste in dissimuliren"; a Latin pamphlet stirs the court; papers
"Schwarzenbergio transmittenda"; outward respect for the Emperor nowhere greater than at Glückstadt; the late Count
Stralendorf "hat sich betrogen". About 55% coherent, the Latin weakest.
