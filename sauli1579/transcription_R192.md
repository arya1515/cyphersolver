business; the King was very content and showed himself much obliged to His Holiness for the warning; he kisses
the feet; the King sent Idiaquez (secretary Juan de Idiáquez) to inform the legate of everything so he could
report to His Holiness; waiting for news of what happens. New paragraph: "In questo punto la sera delli 5? e venuto
il s.r Idiaquez a nome di S.M.ta a dirmi ..." about the Marquis of Ayamonte (former governor of Milan) and the
Anselmo affair; the King is sure it displeased His Holiness; the King will not procure or consent to anything

## Page 2 (IMG_R192_I1232_P2; old fol. 119)

Clear-text summary: continuation. The King says (Idiáquez reports) that he does not wish Italy disturbed and
that for the quiet of Castile everything must be done to prevent any perturbation; he will employ all his forces
if needed. Idiáquez adds that the Marquis [Ayamonte] with those Anselmi [had] fomented [the business], and that the
Huguenots of the Dauphiné [were to aid them] in Italy; the late Duke of Savoy is mentioned (the Marquis was his
friend); the King again kisses the Pope's feet for the warning, showing that such practice is ended; "la pratica
d'Inghilterra" as written to V.S. Ill.ma; the resolution sent earlier; Idiáquez promised the "applicato"; the
exclusion of the general enterprise and the aid of 4000 persons; Mons. Nuntio [Sega?] and others say that the
revocation of the release of Don Antonio['s partisans?] caused such interventions; the army of S.M.ta being much
diminished, the writer does not believe more than 5 or 6 thousand persons will arrive, and that the kingdom has
need of a great part; the negotiation is concluded ("si indussi il negotio ultimamente").

## Summary

- Two texts: pp.1-4 a letter (Idiáquez's audience, the Ayamonte/Anselmi Piedmont plot, the Italian troops for
  Terceira/England); pp.5-6 headed "Copia", dated "li 29 di Novembre 1580" (year reads 1580, not 1581).
- About 73 cipher passages (p1 14, p2 18, p3 17, p4 7, p5 19, p6 8; rough count, several spanning 2-4 lines).
  All have contemporary interlinear glosses apart from two tails: 1.8 ("0?9 32 0 9 75 71 63", after the gloss
  "dubbio che ha S.S.ta", not decrypted) and 6.1 ("[pa]gati", which continues a glossed word).
- New code words beyond the key (proposed): 30 = S.S.ta (x3, firm); 36 = Francia; 40 = cattolici (x2; but 5.8 "in
  una 40" had its gloss "guerra" struck out); 16 = Inghilterra (x4, firm); 72 = denari (6.3, 5.12?) or Regina
  (4.2); 82 = navi or d'Inghilterra (4.2; key says "Hunt(?)"); 10 = Italia (2.1, 2.8, 6.7) but glossed
  V.S. Ill.ma once (6.5); 20 (or 28, second digit overwritten on p.3) = S.M.ta (1.7, 1.12, 2.16) but also
  glossed "Italiani" (3.1, 3.16, 5.1, 5.19). The 20/28 and 10 readings need a second look at higher magnification.
- Numbers are written as plain numerals between 9s (4 mesi, 5 in 6 milia, 3 mesi).
