# Norreys to Cecil, Paris, 9 March 1567/8 — the three ciphered items (DECODE R9251, Add MS 4136 f.161)

Key: Cecil-Norris cipher, 1568 (Tomokiyo's reconstruction from *Cabala*, 1663). Grades H/C/M/I.

(1) ꝺ (turned) — the [French] King (H: same name sign as in 6 Feb 5, 13 and 9 Feb 5)
(2) crossed arrowhead, o, loop, ∞ — "a t ? l", not read. Cabala p. 152 shows the arrowhead is not p
    ("Pope" withdrawn); "Catholic" withdrawn too.
(3) ∂ — the Queen [of Scots]? (I: by position against "for the delivery of the Queen")

Context (CSP Foreign viii no. 2054): "Upon M. Montmorency's first return from the treaty holden at Longjumeau the
King despatched four gentlemen, viz., to the Pope, the Emperor, the King Catholic, and the Swiss … Thinks if
peace be concluded some attempt will be made into Scotland for the delivery of the Queen."
