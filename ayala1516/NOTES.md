# Diego López de Ayala (Brussels) to Cardinal Cisneros, 30 August 1516

Status: read in part (21 Sept 2026)

- AGS Estado leg. 496 fol. 22, DECODE R9954 (8 images, "Non-decrypted"). Catalogue entry from the DECODE refill, scored by rule.
- Images (git-ignored, `img/`): DECODE P1 = AGS 0001, a clear page in Spanish. The cipher runs over AGS 0002–0006
  (DECODE P8, P2, P3, P6, P4). AGS 0007 is blank and 0008 is the address leaf, endorsed with the date 1516 and "Cardenal de España".
- There is no interlinear, marginal or separate decipherment in the record.

## Prior art

- Tomokiyo, cryptiana `spanish2C` (cached as `vasto1527/prior/spanish2C_now.htm`) says: "From the endorsement, it seems
  this was deciphered in the nineteenth century. The cipher is the same as for R10024." Tomokiyo rebuilt the key from
  R10024 (Ayala to the "Cardenal Gobernador", Brussels, 12 July, AGS Estado 12/233, deciphered in the nineteenth
  century), but printed no reading of R9954. The key figure is saved as `img/tomokiyo_key.png` and transcribed in `KEY.md`.
- No printed decipherment of this letter has been found. The Real Academia de Toledo volume *Documentos inéditos de la
  época del Cardenal Cisneros (1516–1517)* prints (no. 36, AGS CSR leg. 394) a different, clear letter of Ayala to
  Cisneros of the same day, recommending Pero Ruiz de Alarcón; the cipher letter is not in it. Gayangos & de la Fuente
  1867 prints Cisneros's letters *to* Ayala, not Ayala's replies.

## Reading

Tomokiyo's key reads the letter unchanged. The first line of AGS 0002 gives "cerca delos oficios que estan vacos en estos reynos".

The reading is in `plain_0002_0003.txt` and `plain_0004_0006.txt`, line by line with [?] marking doubtful letters.

## Outcome: read in part (about nine tenths)

A second pass at 2x with contrast read AGS 0003 lines 18–26 (the page has 26 lines, not 31) and most of the weak lines
of 0002, 0004 and 0006. What is open is listed at the end.

Transcriptions: `plain_0002_0003.txt` and `plain_0004_0006.txt`, done by two image passes per page (first pass,
then "== REVISED ==" re-reads at 2x with contrast). The sign groups are kept beside the readings.

### Key corrections to Tomokiyo's table
- Φ (circle with vertical stroke) = **m**, confirmed on every page (comendador, mucho, amistad, malamente). The hook sign is **j** (carvajal, aconseja, junto consejo).
- The I-shaped sign is a variant of **s** (sobre, se las). A horizontal looped sign (∝) is also **s** (esto se mirara).
- X· = que, and also q inside words (aqui, requerile). 卄 and H both serve for f/h. H· = Xebres (Chièvres): "v. s. dize mal de H·".
- Abbreviations: n[uest]ro señor, su a[lteza], v. s. (vuestra señoría).

### Content (summary)
- **AGS 0002.** Offices in Castile are being sold at court to vile men. Grants already made from Flanders: a regimiento of Toledo to the Marqués de Villena (was Baeza's), one of Plasencia to Rodrigo Niño, one to a nephew of Carvajal, and grants to Vasco de Acuña. Ayala urges that no letter or cédula from here override Cisneros.
- **AGS 0003.** Grants at court go by importunity. Charles is glad of the peace in Castile, with the Condestable and the Duke of the Infantado at one with Cisneros. Queen Germaine is dealing with the French and over a marriage. The Arévalo negotiation. A man writing "mil mentiras", and why Cisneros was given such authority. The power over the Orders of Calatrava, Santiago and Alcántara was sent so that he might remedy them.
- **AGS 0004.** The Comendador mayor of Calatrava protested against Cisneros's power. "Málaga nos a puesto en mucha confusión": a commission with 200 signatures. Don García de Padilla. Disobedience to be punished. A marriage with the Duke of Alba. "V. s. dize mal de Xebres" is reported at court.
- **AGS 0005.** Peace between the King and (probably) France is concluded and to be proclaimed. The king held council on Saturday and resolved to leave for Zeeland and embark. He will be at Antwerp on 15 September. Secretary Barroso arrived six days earlier. Ayala gave his credence and required that the departure be stopped.
- **AGS 0006.** A request for a cédula letting Cisneros appoint presidents, and on the corregidor of Toledo. Ayala has named Don Alonso Téllez and a Don Pedro. He gave Cisneros's credence to the king alone. "El y el chanciller son una persona" (Chièvres and Chancellor Sauvage). A close urging friendship "entre v. s. y el que la desea… servicio a dios y al rey". There is no signature in cipher.

Open: 0004 l.7–8, 23, 25–26 (single words); 0005 l.18 end; 0006 l.8 surname, l.17/19 openings; 0002 l.5 and 12 single words.
