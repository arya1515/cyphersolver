## How the readers' aliases split (pilot lines, 24 lines)
- `ǂ` splits into four shapes by eye at crop scale: **Of** (long, descending, hooked top) = O; **Up** (short, looped foot) = U/V;
  **Pn** (straight, no hook) = P; **F** (looped p-top) = F. Of vs Up is the only pair that is sometimes hard (short Of,
  tall Up); marked `{Of|Up}` 4 times in 24 lines. Pn once stood where R was needed (P16 L12 "trouveroit").
- `Δ` before `£` is **Q** (larger, open, caret+a) in every QUE/QUI of the pilot; plain small triangle = I.
- `✱` barred-star signs in P16 L6 are strike-throughs (a deleted run), not letters.
- `7` = N and B in identical shape (baron, Blancart, Lombardie): not separable by shape; B only by context.
- `4` C vs `27` R: compact 27 looks like 4; separable at 2x in most cases but not all (P8 L3 "liberte").
- `X`: saltire = L; in R4240 "nunce", "Clement", "con" an X-like sign stands for N (the key's curly-x N).
- hooked 5 = G is distinct from 5 = D in shape (P16 L6 "clerge", L12 "seigneur", C7 "faigne").
