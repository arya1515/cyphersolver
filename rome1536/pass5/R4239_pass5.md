# R4239 pass 5: Mâcon to Montmorency, Rome, 6 April 1537 (BnF fr. 3053 ff. 35r-38v)

Gallica ark btv1b90601432: f35r = view 69, f35v = 70, f36r = 71, f36v = 72, f37r = 73, f37v = 74, f38r = 75, f38v = 76
(natives in `gallica/`; map appended to `gallica_views.md`).
- Letter 1 = ff. 35r-35v (DECODE P2-P8).
- Letter 2 opens on f. 36r (P10) with the interlinear gloss "quant au faict de florence" over its first cipher run.
- **f. 37r-v (P18-P24) is a contemporary clear copy of the f36r-v cipher.** I checked this: f37r begins "chevalier de Rhodes et
grec de nation, serviteur du cardinal Salviati, lequel a ce que m'a dict le cardinal Ridolphy ...", which is f36r L07-L18.
- f. 38r = 14 cipher lines, then clear news, the date "A Rome le vj jour d'avril 1537" and the signature.
- f. 38v = P30 postscript with its own interlinear decipherment (**not redone in this pass**).

Method: the same as R4234 pass 5.
- Baselines fitted per page (8 strips, peak tracking, quadratic fit).
- Each line straightened and cut into 3 overlapping segments at native resolution (scratchpad `r4239/seg.py`).
- Transcribed by shape with the catalogue IDs (`R4239_trans.txt`).
- Direct key map, then the 5-gram LM beam (`shapedec.py` functions, fr-1600-letters), then lexicon word division.

Every transcribed line was LM-checked.

Contamination: I saw the head of R4239.md, R4239_pass3.md P3 L1-L16 and the pass-4 P2 lines before transcribing. All other
lines were transcribed blind of the old readings.
