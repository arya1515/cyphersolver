# Dupuy 44 (Mâcon to the King, Rome 15 Feb 1536 o.s. = 1537) vs our fr. 3053 readings (to Montmorency, Feb 1537)

Source: dupuy44_transcription.md (this folder). Ours: origin/main rome1536/R4235*.md, R4234*.md.
Overall: the King's letter is a companion despatch of the same day(s) (R4235 is dated "xv[e] jour de febvrier 1536",
the Dupuy letter "le xve[?] jour de febvrier 1536", both signed "C. Carl de Mascon"). The two letters share several
news items, but they are different texts: Mâcon tells the King the formal business (congregation for peace, the
Turkish truce, the décimes, Pole, Florence) and tells Montmorency the secret side (Farnese designs on Milan,
Pier Luigi's troops, his informers). So the Dupuy text is a content crib, not a verbatim one, except for one sentence.

## 1. Pier Luigi to Parma and Piacenza at the Emperor's request — the only near-verbatim sentence
- Dupuy f.31 (decipherment of f.31bis): "... comme l'allée de Pierre Loys a Parme et Plaisance est suyvant la
  req[ues]te que luy en faict l'Empereur, dont je ne fauldray parler a Sa Saincteté avant le partement dudict sr.
  Pierre Loys. Mays je suis contrainct d'ung peu differer pour ne donner a penser qu'il soit venu a ma congnoissance
  par le moyen dud. sr. Tryvulce ..." ; f.30v: messire Ambroyse had been with the Emperor "en Provence".
- R4235 P3 L21-24: "que l'allée du sieur Pier Loys a Parme et Plaisance est suyvant la requeste que [?] faire
  l'Empereur a Sa Saincteté par messire Ambrois, a son retour de Provence".
- **Proposed correction**: L22 end / L23 start `Δ£o | 7 ǂ o Δ 6 | G | ǂ + Δ 27 o` "que N?EIS-T FAIRE" -> read
  **"que luy [en] a fait[?]/ne[?]... faire"**: the King's version has "que luy en faict l'Empereur", so the doubtful
  group `7 ǂ o Δ 6 G` should be tested as a verb/pronoun of ~6 letters before "faire" (e.g. "a voulu", "en feist",
  "luy fist"); the sense is certain: "la requeste que [l'Empereur] a fait faire a Sa Saincteté par messire Ambrois".
  Also confirms "Ambrois" = Ambrogio Ricalcati and "Provence" (Ricalcati's embassy to Charles V in Provence, 1536).
- Note the source difference: to Montmorency the source is "les espies qu'il [Carpi?] a en la court de l'Empereur";
  to the King it is Trivulzio, shown the Emperor's demands by Ambrogio. Same fact.

## 2. The Milan strongholds
- Dupuy f.30v (Trivulzio's report): the Emperor's "rudesse ... mesmement des retentions que luy avoit dict vouloir
  faire des villes et places fortes du duché de Milan"; Cesarini: the Emperor willing "bailler a monseigneur
  d'Angolesme [d'Orléans] la duché de Mylan" but must "se pouvoir asseurer de vous".
- R4235 P2 L1-2: "le pape tend affin d'avoir les fortes places du duché de Milan entre ses mains"; R4234 P8-P9:
  "ledict pape pourroit persuader l'Empereur de luy bailler le duché de Millan ... mectre dans les villes et places
  fortes dudict duché de Millan".
- Confirms "fortes places / villes et places fortes du duché de Milan" (wording identical). R4235 L1 "AFAN(?)" ->
  **"affin"** is fine.
- **Proposed correction, R4234 P2 L7 and L13 "au dict de post[?]" / "audict de post[?]"**: read **"audict
  depost"** (= dépôt): the Emperor's condition that Milan's strongholds be held in deposit/retention — the
  "retentions ... des places fortes" of the King's letter. "pour induire le Roy a consentir audict depost" and "qu'il
  n'est d'opinion que le Roy doibve jamais consentir audict depost" then make full sense.

## 3. Cesarini's mission to the Emperor
- Dupuy f.30-30v: "le R[everendissi]me Cesarin, qui avoit esté a Naples vers l'Empereur ... avoit prié l'Empereur de
  vouloir bailler a monseigneur d'Angolesme la duché de Mylan".
- R4234 P2 L8-9: "ce que depuis a[?] le cardinal Cesarin es[?] mesmes termes qu'il avoit faict a monsieur [...]".
- **Proposed**: read "ce que depuis a [dict] le cardinal Cesarin, es mesmes termes qu'il avoit faict a monsieur
  [le cardinal Trivulce?/de ...]" — the congregation scene (Cesarini reports, Trivulzio replies) is the "depuis".

## 4. The three décimes and the Pope's neutrality
- Dupuy f.35 + f.35v leaf + f.36/36v clear: the Pope complains that Carpi wrote "que vous aviez n'agueres levé sur
  vostre clergé encores troys decymes de vostre auctorité, sans permission"; the Emperor, passing through Rome,
  had said neither he nor his predecessors ever levied décimes without the Holy See; if the Pope did not react "il
  estoit deliberé ... faire le semblable"; Mâcon answers that last year's décimes were "pour la deffence de vostre
  royaume"; the Pope "estoit pour observer jusques a la mort sa neutralité"; advice to use "les plus doulx et
  gracieux propoz ... audict sr. de Carpy et a Cesar de Nobles ... pour ne donner occasion ... de se departir de sa
  neutralité".
- R4234 P16 (via P15 clear draft): "que le Roy a de nouveau demandé au clergé de son royaume trois decimes, venues a
  la notice de Sa Saincteté, et joinct les trois autres levées l'année passée sans l'octroi d'icelle Sa Saincteté,
  et aussi la calomnie du Turc, soient pour luy faire prendre l'occasion de se declarer contre le dict seigneur".
- Same fact (three new décimes + last year's), same worry. Confirms our reading of P16 "trois decimes ... sans
  l'octroi" ("octroyées" / "octroyast" are the King's-letter words too).
- R4235 P3 L16-18 "me semble, soubz la correction du Roy et vostre, qu'on ne doibt desesperer le dict pape ne
  monstrer semblant d'estre malcontent de luy" = the King's-letter advice ("plus doulx et gracieux propoz ... pour ne
  donner occasion ... de se departir de sa neutralité"). Confirms "desesperer" and "semblant".
- R4234 P11 "il n'est [besoin de?] monstrer semblant a monsieur le cardinal de Carpi": consistent with the King's
  letter's advice to handle Carpi gently; supports **"il n'est [besoing] monstrer semblant"** (the King's letter spells
  "besoing", f.38 "il n'estoit ja besoing").

## 5. The Turkish truce ("la calomnie du Turc")
- Dupuy f.32/f.33: the Pope, informed by Carpi, calls the King's truce with the Turk a "confederation"; Mâcon
  defends it as only a three-year prorogation of the truce made under Clement VII.
- R4234 P16 "et aussi la calomnie du Turc" — confirms the reading "calomnie du Turc" (it is the truce affair).

## 6. Cardinal Pole's legation to England
- Dupuy f.31bis v: the Pope sends "par vostre royaume le legat d'Angleterre", Mâcon gives him letters of
  recommendation to the captains in Piedmont; f.38/f.37: Pole's man back from England, the King's council
  warns Pole not to publish his bull, Henry VIII lifted the seizure of his goods.
- R4235 P10 clear: "... pareillement a Rome en faveur du cardinal Paula qui va legat en Angleterre". Same news.
  "Paula" = Pole ("Paule" in the Dupuy hand).

## 7. Florence (Vitelli, the exiles at Bologna)
- Dupuy f.31bis v: letters of cardinal Ridolfi vs cardinal Gaddi on Florence; the exiles (Filippo Strozzi, cardinals
  Salviati, Ridolfi, Gaddi) gathered at Bologna; "Alexandre Vitelly avoit mys troys cens espaignolz dans ladicte
  ville"; the King's envoy d'Esturtz[?] conferring with them.
- R4235 P8D + P6 clear: "si le Roy est fort en Italie du costé de Lombardie, facillement ledict estat de Florence se
  pourra reduire en l'ancienne liberté ... et n'estant secouru ledict [?]" — P6 clear decipherment "ledict Vitelle(?)".
- **Proposed correction R4235 P8D L5 last group `ǂ Δ Ӿ o ☐ o [?]`**: read **VITELLE** (ǂ=V, Δ=I, Ӿ=T, o=E, ☐=LL,
  o=E): "et n'estant secouru ledict Vitelle", Alessandro Vitelli, commander of the Florence citadel for Cosimo,
  exactly the man named in the King's letter. This removes the "(?)" in the P6 transcription too.
- R4234 P5/P21 (Cosimo de' Medici, Salviati, Strozzi, marriages with Pier Luigi's sons) is the same Florence file.

## 8. Messire Ambrogio (Ricalcati)
- Dupuy f.30v: Ambroyse "luy eschappa a dire" that had the King answered the nuncio's letters, the Emperor would have
  been more pliable; margin f.31bis: another slip of Ambrogio's to Trivulzio. The King is told Ambrogio is
  unreliable.
- R4234 P16: "Le dict messire Ambroise est confirmé du tout impérial ... car il est corruptible plus que tous les
  hommes que je congnoisse"; R4234 P12 clear "Ambroyse ... pension ... pour recompense du bon tour". Consistent.

## 9. Pier Luigi's marriage negotiations
- Dupuy f.31 "que je ne fauldray parler a Sa Saincteté avant le partement dudict sr. Pierre Loys".
- R4234 P12: "la confession que m'a faicte icelluy pape des propoz ... audict Pierre Luis du mariage de son dict
  filz, encores qu'il l'ait voulu pallier et desguiser comme j'escripz au Roy par mes deux [lettres?]" — the
  "escripz au Roy" is confirmed as the Dupuy letter series. The Dupuy text itself does not describe the pope's
  confession in the parts that are clear/deciphered here, so "[lettres?]" is not settled by it (probably
  "depesches" or "lettres": no crib).

## 10. Dom Ferrante Gonzaga and the Dauphin's poisoning
- Dupuy f.34v-f.36: Ferrante Gonzaga arrived in Rome "mardy au soir", the Pope told him the Emperor was in the wrong;
  the Emperor had sent a gentleman to clear himself of the poisoning of the late Dauphin; Gonzaga offers combat;
  leaves "demain ou samedy". No counterpart found in our R4234/R4235 readings (possible content for the unread
  R4235 P7 / R4234 P13-P14).
