## Key notes (this letter)
- Confirms R4240: plain `x` = L and N; `Ӿ` (curly saltire) = T (but reads N in "consentiroient"); crossed `7` = N,
  plain `7` = B ("bon lieu", "ou bien", "ensemble", "non seulement"); `6` = S; `5` = D and, hooked, G.
- `4` is C but also T where the "27" R-sign is written compactly: "attendu", "ceste cause", "trois", "certaine".
- The long barred stroke is O (conte, dont) or U/V (au, pouvoit, avoit) depending on the loop: the two signs are
  hard to separate in this hand; the loop-topped one is F ("fuste", "filz", "faire").
- The bow-tie written like a "q" is V ("avoit", "vouldroit"); the true "q with crossbar" is M ("mandé", "moy").
- `Ω`-like sign = X ("deux jours"); `ꝏ`/`∞` = S; `☐` = LL; `λ` = I/J ("je", "joindre"); `Ꭿ` = Q ("que").
- Code groups: `{30}` pape (x3), `{40}` roy (x3), `{20}` l'Empereur (x3) - all three as in macon_key.md.
- No contemporary interlinear or marginal decipherment anywhere in this record.
