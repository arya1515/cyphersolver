## Key notes (pass 3)

- `ƀ` (ring/6 with a bar - the ring-with-cross E of the key) is the most overloaded sign in P3-P15: **E** (most often),
  **H** (duché, chose, chevalier, chifre, antichambre), **SS** (aussi, passant, laisse, vieillesse, dessoubz),
  **Z** (Albizis), **S** (desmeuvent). Read it by context.
- `ʍ` (M-like sign) = **U/V** (visiter, autrement, voulsist, peur, peust, divertir) - never M in these pages.
- `ᵐ₅` = U/V as in the key; `N` (big curly X) = U/V (usant, fust, sur, seur, quelqu'une).
- `∩` = **P** (pour, pratique, Pierre) and **F** (Ridolfi, faire, fauldrois). `4` = C, and **F** in "refuser",
  "offre" (P11 l. 11?), "affaires" (P4 l. 13); `Δʒ` = FF in "affaire" (P14 l. 4).
- `S` (long 5) = D and **G** (gaigner, Boulongne, ung). `7` = N and **B** (semblant, Boulongne, Urbin, bien,
  dessoubz, chambre). `X` = L and **N** (consentement, P15 l. 7).
- `Γ` (small hooked r) = C in "accepter" (P12 l. 2, P15 l. 5) and "occasion" (P3 l. 7).
- `Ӿ` = T throughout, once I (certain, P6 l. 4). `Ξ` = RR (Pierre, pourroit, Novarre). `ω` = PP (oppinion)
  and once S (voulsist, P12 l. 11).
- Nulls: `ɼ`, `E` (stemmed), `2)` big loop, `Q`-loop, mostly at line ends; `2)` is Z in "dictz"/"potentatz".
- Code groups: `{20}` l'Empereur (P6 x4, P11, P12), `{30}` pape (P3, P6 x2, P7 x2, P14 x3, P15 x2), `{40}` roy
  (P3 x3, P7, P12 x3, P15). **No other numbers.**
- Contemporary gloss: one word, **"donna"**, interlinear over P3 l. 9 (`S o 7 Ӿ 4` = DONNA, Ӿ = N, 4 = A here?).
  No marginal decipherment on any of these pages. The clear text of P18-P24 (R4239b) paraphrases P11-P15 and is the
  practical crib.
- Overlaps: P4 ll. 1-7 = P3 ll. 11-17; P7 ll. 1-3 = P6 ll. 14-16; P12 ll. 1-2 = P11 ll. 12-13; P15 ll. 1-3 = P14
  ll. 14-16; P15 ll. 12-15 = R4239b's P16 ll. 1-4. P10's last two lines are P11 ll. 1-2.
