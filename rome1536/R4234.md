## Key notes (second pass, P2/P3/P5/P6)

- The R4239 correction holds throughout: the big curly `ꭓ` is **U/V** (TROUVER, AVOIT, VOUS, ULIERS, SUISSES,
  PEUVENT) and, with the ǂ/£ family, forms the QUE group; it is never N here.
- `Δ` is **I and Q** (QUIL, QUELQUE, QUE, but also DICT, AINSI); `λ` is I/J (JAMAIS, JEHAN, ITALIENS).
- The ǂ/£ barred-stroke family is, as elsewhere, the whole difficulty: in these four pages it reads
  **C** (a-c-onsentir), **F** (faire, faict, fondement), **H** (chose, chevaulx, chefs), **M** (semble, m'a faict),
  **N** (personne), **O** (vouloir, trouver), **P** (propoz), **U** (luy, du), **V** (vueille, envers).
  Only French context separates them.
- `∩` (tall hairpin) is **P** (pour, propoz, plus, partement, Pisan, Plaisance) but clearly **F/FF** in
  "a(ff)aire" (P2 L5-6, L17) and "(f)rere", "(f)aire" (P5 L14-15): two similar shapes, or one sign doing both.
- `Ξ` (triple bar on a stem) = **RR**, confirmed by PIE-RR-E (Pierre Luis, three times).
- `☐` = LL (icelluy, vueille, famil(l)ier, elle); `Ω`/tailed sign = X (l'ex-pectation, chevaulx);
  `∞` = S; `Ƨ` = Z (escuz, dictz, quilz); `ʒ` = A/U; `b` = E; `g` = E, G and once Q (quelque).
- `Ӿ` (curly saltire) = T (toute, tout, avoit) and **N** (ennemi, personne, maintenant): both, as in R4235.
- `7` = N (crossed) and **B** (possible, doibve, bien, nombre); `4` = C, R and T; `5` = D and, hooked, G
  (assignation, gens, grande); `X` = L, N and, in "de Rodes" (P2 L11), **R**.
- Code groups: `{20}` l'Empereur (P2 L2, P5 L6), `{30}` pape (P2 L5, L13; P3 L7; P6 L9), `{40}` roy (P2 L2, L7,
  L12; P5 L14; P6 L6). **No number other than 20/30/40 anywhere in P2, P3, P5, P6.**
- Contemporary decipherment: none on these four pages (no margin, no interlinear). The anchors here are instead the
  scribe's own clear sentences left in the middle of the cipher (P3 twice, P5 once, P6 once).
