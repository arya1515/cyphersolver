# R9649 — Lope Hurtado de Mendoza (Rome) to Charles V, 9 November 1522

RAH Salazar 9/26, ff. 266–268. DECODE R9649 (3 openings).

- **f. 266r** — the letter: *S. C. Mat*, clear text with three cipher runs; dated *De Roma .ix. de noviembre*,
  signed (autograph) Lope Hurtado. Endorsed on the address leaf *Ala S. C. C. Mat Del emperador y Rey señor*.
- **f. 266v / 267** — show-through and the address; no further cipher.
- **f. 268r** — headed *Al Rey — De Lope hurtado de ix de noviembre*: **a contemporary clear version of the
  whole letter** (the clerk's decipherment). This is a crib for every cipher run.
- Not a duplicate of R9644/R9648 (1 Nov, long letter), R9650 or R9656: a separate short note of the same
  day as R9648's despatch. It carries its own crib, so it needed no outside alignment.

Notation: 1524 sign names (`lopehurtado1523/key_1524.tsv`), 1522 codes (`key_codes.tsv`). New sign names
used here: `ϑ` = the open-topped 8-like sign that stands for t (distinct from the closed `8` = e; the two
look alike below 2x zoom), `δ` = the plain delta sign (s), `Ꮒ` = the looped mark that opens a run,
`ɣ̊` = ɣ with a small o under it, `ʙ` = the barred double-stroke b, `Ho`, `H`, `Ɛ` as they look.

## Cipher runs, transcription and decode

### Run 1 (f. 266r ll. 1–2) — clear: *...de su parte como* [cipher] *.* [cross-reference ⑨]

```
Ꮒ   yub zuf      zar  ϭ 7 4 ϑ Ho   y ʇʇ ∠ ♀   xil  ʇ Ho ϑ ß   top  & δ b α 7
∅   el  Cardenal de   s a n t a    c r u z    le   m a  t a   por  o s t i a
```
= *como el Cardenal de Santa Cruz le mata por Ostia* — exact count against f. 268 word for word.

### Run 2 (f. 266r ll. 5–8) — clear: *...que mandasse poner gran recabdo* [cipher]

```
Ꮒ yω ʃta   yuc xug yan        zar  & δ ϑ α ß   ʑ  ton tu xug zar ß  xur x α 8
?  ?  ?    en  la  fortaleza  de   o s t i a   y  que no la  de  a  na  d i e
top ton  tu tun      zun ʒ ʃu xug   ti   ϑ 8 4 oo ʇʇ   Ɛ H   ton ⅋ & δ m
por que  no querria  da  r se la    ni   t e n e  r    l e   que x o s o
```
= *[…] en la fortaleza de Ostia y que no la de a nadie, porque no querria darsela ni tener le quexoso.*
Every token after `Ꮒ yω ʃta` aligns one-to-one with f. 268. `yω ʃta` has no counterpart in the clear
version (the clerk writes *gran recaudo en la fortaleza*), so it is either nulls or a word the clerk
dropped; `ʃta` also opens a run on R9646 and R9656 (`∂6 ʃta`), which points to a filler.

### Run 3 (f. 266r ll. 10–11) — clear: *En este punto he sabido* [cipher] *es venido aqui, embarcose en Barcelona...*

```
Ꮒ  ton yub  Ɛ ω ɣ oo ƀ α 7 x &     ʙ 8 ɣ̊ z ß ʇʇ 9 α 4 &
∅  que el   l i c e  n(c) i a d o   b e r  n a r  d i n o
```
= *que el licenciado Bernardino* (f. 268: *el licen[cia]do Bernardino*). `Bernardino` is an exact
ten-for-ten slot. `licenciado` has nine signs for ten letters: either `ƀ` covers *nc* or the writer
spelled *licenado*/*licençiado* short.

## Running reading (the letter as enciphered)

> Su Beatitud me ha mandado que escriva a v. Mt. de su parte como **el Cardenal de Santa Cruz le mata
> por Ostia**, que como le ha menester por ser mas antiguo en el colegio, que querria que v. Mt.
> escriviesse a su Santidad que mandasse poner gran recabdo **en la fortaleza de Ostia, y que no la de a
> nadie, porque no querria darsela ni tener le quexoso.**
> En este punto he sabido **que el licenciado Bernardino** es venido aqui; embarcose en Barcelona a v. del
> passado. Dizenme que esta escondido en casa de un su hermano. Luego aviso al duque para ver lo que le
> parece. V. Mt. escriva lo que manda... De Roma .ix. de noviembre [1522].

Adrian VI asks Charles V to write urging him to garrison Ostia and give the fortress to no one: Cardinal
Santa Cruz (Bernardino Carvajal, cardinal-bishop of Ostia as dean of the College) is pressing for it by
seniority, and the Pope wants neither to hand it over nor to offend him. The *licenciado Bernardino* who
landed from Barcelona and is hiding at his brother's house is left unidentified.

## Coverage
