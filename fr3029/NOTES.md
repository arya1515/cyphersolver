# BnF fr. 3029, ciphered letters filed with La Trémoille's papers (DECODE R3666–R3687) — NOTES

Status: no write-up

**Verdict: already broken by others. Nothing to add.** Catalogue entry 174 ("Unknown sender to unknown recipient,
20 ciphertexts, 11 Jan 1530") is resolved and removed. Checked in one session on 2026-09-21; images not viewed.

## Prior solution

George Lasry broke this cipher in 2023, and Norbert Biermann independently (private communication), as reported by
Satoshi Tomokiyo, cryptiana, "George Lasry's decipherments" (GL.htm, section "De la Tremoille's(?) Cipher
(??1520-1521): BnF fr.3029, BnF fr.3092"; local copy `gramont1529/GL.htm` l. 255 ff.) and francis.htm.

- Tomokiyo's list of letters in the cipher, fr. 3029: f.67 (no.34), ff.100 and 105 (no.49, Latin), f.134 (no.59,
  "au roy"), f.162 (no.66), ff.172/176 (no.68), f.182 (no.70), f.186 (no.72); also fr. 3092 ff.101, 103, 107.
- The 20 DECODE records are fr. 3029 ff. 67, 100, 105, 162, 164, 166, 168, 172, 174, 176, 182, 186, 188, 190,
  191, 193, 195, 197, 199, 202 (record names `BNF_Français_3029_<folio>`): the same letters and their continuation
  leaves. f.134 has no DECODE record in this group.
- Tomokiyo prints readable decipherments of f.67 (the Hungarian ambassador, the Cardinal "fort variable", war in
  Hungary) and f.186 (elective privileges of Cluny, Prémontré and the Bordeaux province churches), and fr. 3092
  f.100.
- Dating: Tomokiyo gives ??1520–1521 (possibly the earliest French cipher letters); DECODE's "11 Jan 1530" is
  unsupported by either. Not resolved here.

## Open

Full readings of the remaining leaves (ff.162–202) are not printed by Tomokiyo; they would need Lasry's key and the
BnF images. Not attempted: the cipher is not ours to claim.
