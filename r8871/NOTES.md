# BL Add MS 32280 f. 3 (DECODE R8871): trigram fragment "Suite du No 13 / No 24"

Status: no write-up (skipped: impossible from the available material. 77 groups of a large/enciphered code, no key on DECODE or in the volume's key records)

Catalogue: "Unknown sender to unknown recipient", BL Add MS 32280 f 3, DECODE R8871 (dated 1723/1842, the span of the volume).

## What the leaf is (viewed 21 Sept 2026, DECODE images, cookie route)

- f. 3r is a ruled grid form, 12 cells per row, filled in a French hand. Headings: "Suite du No 13." above the grid and "No 24."
  in the left margin. It holds 77 groups of three characters: lower-case letters, digits, and the ligature œ
  (e.g. `qlh œ29 k8d qlh uxi tsw ...`). The rest of the grid and f. 3v are blank. Transcription: `f3_transcription.txt`
  (uncertain at b/6, 9/g, 1/l, r/v).
- It is a copy made on a prepared form, probably by or for the Deciphering Branch, not an original letter. No sender,
  recipient, date or decipherment is on the leaf.
- The companion record R8870 (ff. 1–2) is a different document: an Italian-style numeric code letter (groups up to 1647,
  dashes joining groups) ending "Duplicata". It is unrelated to f. 3's system.

## Statistics

77 groups, 76 distinct (only `qlh` repeats, at positions 1 and 4). Character IoC × 35 ≈ 1.12, so the characters are close to
flat. This is not a substitution of letters. The groups are a large code or an enciphered code.

## Key search

- Add MS 32280 is a Deciphering Branch key volume. DECODE has 26 key records from it (R8890, R8894–R8919, ff. 55–116,
  1790–1842). Every image was downloaded and viewed as a contact sheet (`keys_sheet.jpg`, git-ignored). They are numeric
  code tables, mostly Dutch ("Dutch Calculation", "Washington and the Hague", "The Hague and Stockholm"; de Leeuw: Add MSS
  32279–80 hold intercepted Dutch despatches of 1823–42), plus one Italian-style numeric vocabulary (R8890, 1790).
  None uses three-character letter/digit groups. No key for f. 3 is on DECODE.
- The ciphertext is too short (77 groups, one repeat) to rebuild a code without a key or a crib.

## Where it could move

"No 13" (whose continuation this is) and the other numbered pieces of this series would be needed. They are probably
in the unimaged ff. 4–54 of Add MS 32280 or in the neighbouring Branch volumes. A key or decipherment would have to come
from the BL volume itself.
