# Colbert correspondence ciphers (Mélanges de Colbert 127, 168bis, 172) — cryptiana unsolved item #4

Three undeciphered passages, all catalogued in DECODE by Tomokiyo (uploader), status Non-decrypted. Second session
2026-09-16 (Gallica back up): images fetched, letters read, the printed catalogue searched for sibling letters, three
cipher designs tested against matched controls. **Result: the Charost and Gravel passages are one key, not two; the
Charost letter is 1675, not 1673; no other letter in that key exists online; the key is not any alphabetical
letter-and-syllable design of the kind this office used; not solved.** Details below.

## Established this session

1. **b and c are the same key.** Both use plain three-digit groups 52–489 with no diacritics. Of the 55 distinct groups,
   13 are common to both letters (75, 141, 199, 253, 327, 369, 415, 417, 433, 437, 439, 481, 484), including the six
   commonest overall; a four-group phrase `75 282 284 415` recurs inside two Gravel passages; and both letters end a
   sentence on `437 415 48x` (Charost `437 415 483`, Gravel `437 415 484`), while Charost's passage opens with `484`.
   Charost wrote in the same letter that he was sending "la mesme nouvelle" that morning to Maulevrier. Gravel's copy was
   sent to Maulevrier. The key is therefore most likely Maulevrier's cipher of 1674–75, shared with the correspondents
   of his command. Tomokiyo's page and DECODE list them as two unrelated ciphers.
2. **Dates.** Vol. 171bis = June 1675, vol. 172 = July–August 1675 (catalogue). So the "Colbert's(?) cipher (1674)" of
   Chaulnes at 171bis f. 607 is June 1675, and Charost's letter is 3 July 1675. Vol. 169 = Sept–Dec 1674, 170 =
   Jan–Feb 1675; 168bis therefore ≈ August 1674, consistent with Gravel's date.
3. **Context of b.** Charost's letters of 1 July (f. 5), 3 July (f. 23, cipher), 5 July (f. 38, with a clerk's abstract at
   f. 37) form a sequence: 1 July, a man landed with English horses says Charles II is recalling Parliament (prorogued on
   17 June to October, as Charost had reported on 22 June, 171bis f. 682, "les Espagnols et les Hollandois sont frustrés
   de leurs espérances"); 3 July, "une nouvelle … de conséquence pour le service du Roy", from a Calais merchant's
   English correspondent, in cipher, followed in clear by a request to Maulevrier for troops "en cas que je fusse menacé
   de siège"; 5 July, the Parliament news "s'est trouvé faux". The cipher text is the merchant's news; its subject is
   English or Dutch preparations against Calais. No clear-text copy of it was found.
4. **Sibling search (the reason this item was reopened) is complete and negative for what is online.** La Roncière and
   Bondois, *Catalogue des manuscrits de la collection des Mélanges de Colbert*, t. I (Gallica bpt6k2091654; canvas =
   page + 25; vol. 127 at p. 270, vols 165–176 at pp. 478–520) lists every letter by folio. Charost letters: 171bis
   ff. 444, 519, 542, 546, 547, 597, 624, 682; 172 ff. 5, 23, 37, 38, 100, 101, 121, 122, 201, 273, 277, 303, 307, 308,
   319, 320, 322, 323, 325; 172bis ff. 365, 366, 368, 422, 450, 455, 456, 461, 463 (not digitised). Maulevrier to
   Colbert: 171bis ff. 489, 670; 172 ff. 249, 329; 172bis f. 333. R. de Gravel: 171bis f. 487. Every Charost letter
   online (all 19 in 172; ff. 597–598, 624, 682–683 in 171bis, whose Gallica copy covers only ff. ≈570–775) was read:
   **all in clear**, none with cipher or a decipherment; they concern bélandres, materials for the fortifications,
   Ruyter's fleet off the Downs, an officer sent to Dover and Deal (16–18 Aug). The two Maulevrier letters online are of
   August, not July. Clément's *Lettres … de Colbert* (t. III, V, VIII on archive.org) has no reply to Charost of July
   1675 and nothing on the alarm.
5. **Key design.** None of the published Colbert-office keys of 1665–74 (Millet, d'Estrades, Croissy 1668–74, Pomponne,
   Gravel 1672, d'Estrées, Condé 1673, Chaulnes 1675) uses plain three-digit groups above ~316; all but Condé's rely on
   diacritics. Token distribution here: 46 of 106 tokens in the 400s over 17 distinct values (415 ×9, 433 ×6, 417 ×5,
   481 ×5, 466 ×4), 20 in the 300s, 12 in the 200s, 16 in the 100s, 12 two-digit. Four designs were tested with the
   monotone annealer `solve_regions.py` (French 5-gram LM, clear context fixed) against controls of the same 106-token
   shape (`control2.py`):

   | design | controls | target |
   |---|---|---|
   | syllables 52–399 in block order, letters 400–489 alphabetical (`Sa,La`) | 2 of 2 valid controls read (~100 %, ~80 %), seeds agree | 3 seeds disagree, −972 / −996 / −972, unreadable |
   | same with vowels free inside blocks (`Sc,La`) | both controls fail | fails |
   | syllable blocks + free letters (`Sc,Lf`); low letters free + blocks + alphabetical letters (`Lf,Sc,La`) | controls fail | fails |
   | Condé-type single series, letters and syllables interleaved alphabetically 52–489 (`Ma`) | 2 of 2 controls read in full | 3 seeds disagree, −939 / −926 / −912, unreadable |

   So at 106 tokens the method can read either strict alphabetical design and the target is neither; the looser
   designs are below the method's limit at this length. The frequency profile (half the text in seventeen 400-group
   values, a repeated four-group phrase, common sentence-final trigram) is at least as consistent with a **nomenclator
   of words** as with letters, and a word code of this size cannot be broken from 106 groups without a crib.
6. **Item a** is a different, smaller cipher (two-digit groups with overlines, a code number 580). Only three names are
   enciphered. Not attacked.

## What would solve it

- The key itself: Maulevrier's papers, or the Archives des Affaires étrangères / SHD A1 (Louvois) for 1674–75. Ulbert,
  *Geheime Post* (2015), describes ministerial keys of 1661/1675/1676 that are not online.
- Or a clear-text copy of the 3 July 1675 news: Maulevrier's letters to Colbert or Louvois of 4–6 July 1675 (171bis f. 670
  is June; 172 ff. 249, 329 are August), or Louvois's reply in SHD A1 (not online).
- Or more text in the same key: Charost's letters in 172bis (Aug–Dec 1675, not digitised), and any Gravel–Maulevrier
  copies in 168–171 (168, 169, 170, 170bis, 171 are not on Gallica; only 171bis is, and only its second half).

## Access notes (2026-09-16)
- Gallica IIIF manifests and images work with a browser User-Agent; `.texteBrut`, `ContentSearch`, `Toc` and the ALTO
  endpoint return a "Vérification de sécurité" (ALTCHA) page or 400 to scripts, and the host drops connections for
  ~10 minutes after a burst of 429s. Space requests ≥1 s apart.
- DECODE `RecordsList?search=<word>` lists records without login (Charost → R2734 only; Maulevrier → R2733 only; the
  Mélanges de Colbert series is R2668–R2738 plus R2228–R2229).
- BnF Archives et manuscrits has an inventory tree for the Mélanges Colbert (ark cc95411w = vol. 11) but its search is
  JavaScript-driven; the printed catalogue on Gallica is the usable route.
