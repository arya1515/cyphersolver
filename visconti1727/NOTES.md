# BL Add MS 32270 f. 42, DECODE R7931: the Visconti Italian cipher, 1727

Status: explained (not a letter: a key), 21 Sept 2026. Catalogue item 73 (class A), removed.

**Catalogued as.** "Count Visconti (Germany) to Visconti, 1727": DECODE R7931, record type *ciphertext*, "Partially
decrypted", 1 p., homophonic, nomenclator, numerical, Italian. The catalogue assumed a ciphered letter whose
contents were unknown.

**What it is.** Not a letter. Add MS 32270 is a volume of the Deciphering Branch's reconstructed keys (German courts,
ff. 2–295; DECODE R7916–R8048, all typed as keys except R7927, R7929, R7930 and R7931). Folio 42 is the branch's
fair copy of a 300-place numerical nomenclator, the dorse endorsed *Count Visconti at Bruxelles with Marq.s
Visconti here, 1727, Italian Cypher*; a later hand repeats *Count Visconti with Marquis Visconti 1727*. "Here"
is London: this is the cipher of a Count Visconti in Brussels (Austrian Netherlands) with the Marquis Visconti in
London, as the branch rebuilt it from intercepts. The two Viscontis are not identified further here. DECODE mistyped it as a ciphertext;
nothing on the leaf is enciphered text, and so there is nothing to read.

**Sibling records.**
- R7932 (f. 43–44), typed as a key: the branch's working draft of the same key. The same values (1 Walpole,
  3 Maestà, 22 Sinzendorf, 26 Ambasciadore, 33 Rialso, 212 Ministro, 218 Imperadore, 254 Staremberg), with rows of
  dots counting occurrences and a red note at the head.
- R7933 (f. 45–46), typed as a key: endorsed *1727/8 Count Sinzendorff / Count Visconti at Bruxelles / Marq.s
  Visconti here / French Cypher*, a second, French-language key between the same parties.
- No intercepted letter in this cipher is bound in Add MS 32270. Where the branch's decipherments of the
  intercepted letters are now filed was not searched.

**The key (f. 42), transcribed in `key_f42.tsv` (193 filled places).**
- 1–100, 101–144, 160–168: single letters, doubled letters (mm, ff, nn, rr, tt, ss, ll, pp) and CV syllables
  (ga, be, fe…, bu, fu…, bi, fi…, bo, fo…, co, go…), several homophones per vowel (e = 2, 13, 126; a = 4, 37;
  i = 11, 131; u = 78, 94, 114…).
- Names and words: 1 Walpole, 3 Maestà, 21 Vienna, 22 Sinzendorff, 24 Francia, 26 Ambasciadore, 145 Maestà
  Imperiale / Altezza, 146 Ostenda, 152 Inghilterra, 170 Townshend, 171 Ollanda, 177 Richelieu, 184 Londra,
  189 Annover, 192 Spagna, 197 Congresso, 201 Parlamento, 203 Trattato, 208 Commercio, 218 Imperadore,
  220 Parigi, 224 Madrid, 243 America, 247 Pace, 254 Staremberg, 261 Arciduchessa. Ostend, the Congress (Soissons,
  1728), Richelieu (ambassador at Vienna 1725–28) and Commercio fit the Ostend Company crisis of 1727.
- Places 262–300 empty; about 105 places are blank or marked with a dotted underline (the decipherer's doubt), or
  written "p.n. of a place (Amsterdam)", "p.n. of a country". That is the mark of a key recovered from traffic,
  not a copy of the original.

**Grades.** The transcription is H except 33 "Rialso" (C: the reading of the word is unsure; in R7932 too) and 172
(Imperadore struck out, "Reggenza" [?] beside it, C).

**Outcome.** Explained. The catalogue entry is a key record mistyped as a ciphertext. The leaf is the solution,
not the problem. DECODE correction queued: R7931 record type → key, status → N/A (keys carry status 4).
