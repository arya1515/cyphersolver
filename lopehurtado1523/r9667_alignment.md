2 May 1523** (docket "Al Rey — Del abbad de Najera a dos de mayo"), bound beside Lope Hurtado's letter.
Its image is mirrored. Section A is about Alonso Sanchez's letters from Venice, the articles of the
agreement with the Venetians sent to the Infante, and the army's pay; section B is blank. None of
the six cipher runs on f.6r/6v can be its ciphertext (no Venice/Infante context, run A sits inside
a sentence about the Pope's servants). **No exact-count slot exists for R9667**; everything below is
context-only and at best *probable*.

## Derived values (all PROBABLE, context only)
- `4ʃL∂8ɛ` = **provea** (6 signs, 6 letters), slot "que V. Mt ___ ... de beneficios a sus criados"; the stem recurs in D as `4ʃL∂87xm` = prove-a-d-o? (**proveado**/"proveydo"; ends 7xm = a-d-o as in 1522 honrrado/approvechado). CONTRADICTION: 1522 key has `4` = n and ⊃ = p; here `4` would be p. Either the 1523 alphabet differs, or my `4` is a different sign from the 1522 `4` (likely; eye-transcription). Flagged, not adopted.
- `Bens.` = **beneficios**, apparently a clear abbreviation left inside the cipher line (probable).
- Codes simply re-used with the 1522 value, sense-consistent in context: yub=el, taf=papa, tu=no, ton=que, rab=V. Mt, zar=de, xon=mas, yuc=en, yof=esta, xul=lo, ʃu=se/su. None contradicted; none newly confirmed (no exact slot).
- New, unread groups: zo, fu, ti, tudd, xic, yo, yiq, xeg, xard, xuld, xiLd, tin, tas6, fedz, md, oo.
