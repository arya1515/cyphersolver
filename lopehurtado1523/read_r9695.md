## Reading of the cipher
Not read. Only fragments: A02 "…do que V. Md. …"; A10 "es en Italia … que no …"; B02 "el papa no por que…"
(top = por, 1522 key); B03 "…en Francia…" — and here the struck cipher is followed by a clear rewrite "queriendo
beneficios en Francia ni cosa del Rey de Francia", which confirms `yuc yon` = en Francia (already in key) and suggests
`tar`/`tor` = Rey de Francia (key_1524: tor), i.e. B03 `zar tar` is probably zarε tor misread by me.
The draft's cipher is written small and fast over show-through; my sign transcription is the weak point, so the
74 % token coverage (184/249, by script) is mostly single letters that do not assemble into words.
The cipher gaps coincide with the struck/rewritten passages, so a better transcription of page B against its own
clear rewrites is the best route to more values.

## English gist
Lope Hurtado to Charles V, 8 July 1523: the Pope was pleased with the Emperor's letter; complaint that letters have
been so slow; the rumoured poisoning could not be verified, the Pope's and chamberlain's men are still in prison, the
Pope guards his person and eats apart served by old servants; asks that naturalisations/benefits be given to the
Pope's intimates (the chamberlain Pedro de Roma, Francisco, Theodorico); Vincencio de Benedictis not condemned;
on f.340 Hurtado asks for the house of "Federico emperador" or confiscated goods in lieu of the Palermo prisoner.
