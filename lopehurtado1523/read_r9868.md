## English gist

Lope sends Gattinara a copy of his 11 March letter to the Emperor: the archbishop of Capua has left; in his
absence all business hangs on the Datary [Giberti], who stands as close to the Pope as can be. [Cipher: he
needs […], because he was ill-pleased with the past, given the place he holds; Lope thinks it inexpedient,
and every day it seems more necessary to satisfy him …] Benefices have been shared among the Cardinals as
the King of France did with his; since the payment is necessary, the Emperor should consent to it. The
absence of [code zad] from Rome will not help the Emperor's affairs; Gattinara should see that the Emperor
treats [him] so that he comes back more obliged, the sooner the better. f.430v (mostly clear): Bernaldino de
la Barba has gone; the Pope's servants who stayed should be rewarded; some want the Duke [of Sessa] to sue
for naturalizations in Naples; many Cardinals want to be Protector; a long cipher passage (unread) on a
valiant man ("Pasasiano"?) with many sons and nephews not in the Emperor's pay; the Duke has written more.
