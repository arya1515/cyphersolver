## 3. R9667 against the CSP Spain ii no. 548 abstract

548 is a short English abstract of a lost contemporary decipherment, so it gives the order of topics, not wording.
Laid against the runs (R9667 is transcribed in the 1522 sign notation, so its letter signs decode badly with the
1524 key and are not trusted):

| run | clear around it | 548 topic it falls under | what the codes say |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | before "y ya estan sin ninguna esperança" | "Although the Pope has not yet declared himself about his alliance, the French have already lost all hope" | "… el papa no fu[…] de … mas en …": fits "el papa no [se ha declarado]"; `fu`/`zo` open |
| B | before "porque todos estan tan alegres de lo que el papa ha hecho" | "the servants of the Pope were not so much French partisans as it was feared" | `yun` (fran-) in the run fits "franceses"; the rest unread |
| C | after "y lo mismo Theodorico" | "especially Theodoric, who was most suspected" | "que [8ʃ7] de [ɛ tin] mas se de[…]": fits "que era de quien mas se [sospechava]"; not an exact slot |
| D-E | "y que en Flandes … a sus criados" | "the preferment the Pope conferred on them, to which Madame Margaret has objected"; "reward them" | "que el papa ha dicho que V.Md provea … de beneficios": the NOTES reading holds |
| F | before "al presente me parece que esta provision seria necessaria" | "accept as subjects those nearest the Pope" | unread |

The crib confirms the topic order and the reading of runs D-E, but it gives no exact slot, so **no value is
confirmed from it** and R9667's coverage does not move. A real gain would need the contemporary decipherment
the CSP editor used, which is not among the DECODE images.
