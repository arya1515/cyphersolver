## Contradictions and doubts
1. **L**: 1522 key had L = i (downgraded). Here L stands in the r slot of "remedio" (c18, exact 7-sign run).
   One context only; either a different sign or the 1522 value is wrong.
2. **∂**: 1522 key had no ∂. Here a ∂-like sign is p (barred form ∂̶: capua, puede, perdida, pudiendo,
   reputacion, poco, aparejo) and t (tribucion, temen, temiendo), and in salida it stands in the s slot,
   in "canse o concierte" in the o slot. I cannot separate the forms reliably at 2x; p and t are both
   well attested, so there are at least two signs here. Needs a 4x-8x zoom comparison.
3. **ʇʇ** carries r (many), b (triple-crossed form, sure), and v (voto, sobervia). The 1522 transcription
   used ʒ for r; ʇʇ here is probably the same sign as 1522's "#" in ∞7∞ᵷLʒ#8ʃ3m (satisfecho).
4. **m**: o in poco/costa/cosas/aparejo (as 1522), but an underlined m̲ in "se funde" would be f.
5. **z**: n everywhere, but h in "fecho" (xod f z y) — one slot, probable only.
6. **4** in the m slot of "temiendo" (v02) — probably my misreading of ʋ.
7. **φ** in c07 "re-tirar" sits in an r slot — probably a misreading (φ = u/v in ten contexts).
8. **tef**: 1522 tef = paz (confirmed); here tef twice in "nec(esidad)" and once in "tregua o paz antes"
   where it could be paz. Either homophonic code (unlikely) or the two uses are different groups.
9. **ʃob**: segun (v05) and ve- (v10) — one of them wrong.
10. Spots not aligned: c13 "ton xul ton ω ʃʃ 8" (querria), c13 "ßʇʇω7 ℔ yac" (a ello / dello),
    c14 "8 y z ton" (o dize que), v01 "un ʇʇe 7" (querria una), v14 front of "inconveniente".
