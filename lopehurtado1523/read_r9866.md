# R9866 — Lope Hurtado to the Grand Chancellor (Gattinara), Rome, 28 February 1524 (ff. 285r–285v)

Images: `img/IMG_R9866_I45986_P1.jpg` (right = f.285r), `P2` (left = f.285v; right = f.286v, address "Al muy
Ill.e S.or el S.or gran chanciller de la Sacra Cesarea Mt"). Heading "muy Ill.e señor"; the letter says "V. S.".
So the addressee is the Grand Chancellor Mercurino di Gattinara, not the Emperor.

**Date.** The subscription on f.285v, checked at 3.5x, reads "De Roma a xxviij de hebrero": **28 February
1524**, not 20 February. (The year is from the docket on the facing leaf, "1524", and the Beaurain news that
the 3/5 Feb letter R9846 also carries.) The cross-check file `trans_R9866.md` reads the same date and address.

Cipher runs (Rn) token by token in the symbols of `r9846_cipher.txt`. `ℋ` below = the looped double-crossed
sign that I cannot separate from ʇʇ with certainty; I transcribe it as ʇʇ. `T̄` = the barred T sign
(⊤ of the key). Where the other session's transcription (`trans_R9866.md`) and mine differ, mine is given.

## English gist

To Gattinara: Beaurain's coming was timely; Lope has briefed him and Gattinara will see it in his letters.
The French want, by craft or force, to stay on this side of the Alps; [cipher: they have not been harmed in
those parts … the Emperor's armies; losing one … being unable to do anything …]. The Emperor must not lose
the world's respect now that they are leaving it, since he spends so much on the war, having the best
captains and men by land and sea; if he is to rely on this [cipher: money is needed …]. Until the time comes
that [the French no longer hold?] Italy [cipher, unread] … in Naples a commission should go to one person
to [cipher] … Beaurain will report, so Lope only begs Gattinara to read a chapter he wrote to Soria and help
him get paid what he is owed.
