## English gist

Beaurain has been sent money matters by the Pope ("the moneys the Pope offered"); the Pope told Lope that he
would fulfil the rest, pleading his need; Florence has taken no decision yet. Cardinal Cortona and Ippolito
de' Medici leave next week for Florence. The French have lost Fuenterrabía (clear). If the archbishop [of
Capua] arriving at the Emperor finds the army ready to sustain the war here, his report will match what the
Pope sent to offer the Emperor; Charles should see that the archbishop is received so that his […]; given the
need for money here, any help is good, and the archbishop's more than any. The Pope's goodwill and words
could not be better; "God grant that the [deeds] be the same." The rest (Easter news, Hungary and the
Turk, the Legate in Moravia, the Infante) is in clear.
