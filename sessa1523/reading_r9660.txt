Sign notes (this copy):
 - ꝛo (2-like hook over o) = s, mostly plural -s: lo-s, otro-s, todo-s, la-s cosa-s, ordinaria-s, potentado-s.
   This is the key's plain p-loop "ꝑo".
 - ꝺ (6-like) = r throughout (se-r, da-r, o-f-en-de-r, con-se-r-va-r, acorda-r).
 - Ɉ (hooked) = u/v: c-u-y-dado, p-u-e-b-lo, B-u-r-g-u-es, m-a-n-t-u-v-o, a-n-t-i-g-u-a, V-o-l-terra.
 - 9 = l in pueb-l-o, Pando-l-fo, Vo-l-terra (variant); = e in p-e-r-o, P-e-t-r-u-cci, esp-e-rança-s.
 - q = l in "Ɉ z q ott 9 x α" = V-o-l-t-e-r-a.
 - x = r in Volte-r-a; = e elsewhere (per key). Ambiguous.
 - z = o (V-o-l-, c-o, s-o-lo): confirms Pass 14's guess.
 - z y = -on: "ꝛo z y" = son, three times. Confirms Pass 14.
 - tto = b (B-a-l-o-n, b-a-s-t-a, B-u-r-g-u-es, no-b-le-s, pue-b-lo, ace-b-tar).
 - Є = h (he-c-h-o, h-i-z-o; "Є3 Є4" = h-i-j-o, so Є = h and also j).
 - L (capital L sign) = ? ("no da-L-a-r-ia", "lex sad L ꝛo α ott g do"). Open.

 - Volterra (card. Francesco Soderini) is SPELLED: "Ɉ z q ott 9 x α" (P5-L l.6, 13, 16; l.8 variant "Ɉ 4 9 ott 9 x α"). Firm.
