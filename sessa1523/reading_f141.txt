Sense (tentative): Sessa weighs how the Pope (Adrian VI) stands toward the French; he could go to
see His Holiness "with the pretext" of something, finding no disposition to listen, and meanwhile to report.
The clear continues: "Hizose asy. Fuimos en Roma a los xxvj del pasado; entro con muy buen recibimiento, y el posar
fue en palacio ..." (the viceroy of Naples, Lannoy, whose Rome visit of January 1523 this letter reports).
This is a sample, not a reading of the letter: about half the tokens resolve with the 1524 key, the rest need the
token-tile work that sessa1524 used (hand, not key, is the obstacle: the 1523 hand is the same writer's).
