(p.1)
Paris Apr 9th 1670 S.N.

Wee are in hourly expectacion of Mr Godolphin's arrivall
here. Yesterday the King came to Paris much
to the Burgers satisfaction. visited severall Churches
& was present in ye afternoon at a Coremonious
babtisme of Madams young daughter the duchess de
Valois, who had ye Dolphin to Godfather & ye
Queen with Mad^lles to GodMothers.
a Guernsey Merchant dying lately in this place 
his goods were seized on by droit d'aubeine, 
but upon my Ld Ambassadour complains we are
in hopes & King will dispense with what our former
Treatys have granted to the subjects of England.


(p.2)
Another thing I must not omit to impart to your Lo[rdshi]pp 
I hear from good hands That among other 
restoracions made de part et d'autre between the 
French & Algerins upon their late Treaty of 
Peace, ye former putt again into the Pirats hands 
an English Vessell they had retaken certainly 
such an inhumane proceeding cannot bee owned 
by Civil or Christian Laws.

Now ye Kings voyage draws nigh & ye Courtiers 
prepare in earnest. his Troops are upon the place 
by this time; but (his inclinations whatever they bee) 
its generally believed his intentions are not to doe any 
thing but view his new Fortifications wch I am told 
by many of judgement are the best in Europe 
& am promised the models but not without expence.

The merchants of Jersey & Guernsey complain much
of an arrest lately forcing them to sell their 
stockins within 3 days after their coming to Paris, 
or else they shall bee confiscated, an invention to 
break ye neck of that small Trade also, for they 
must sell them to losse not being allowed time for 
their markett the priculas* of this & other petty
greivances of wch they & others complain I will
presume by the next ordinary so transmitt to yr Lo[rdshi]pp
