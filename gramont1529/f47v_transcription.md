## Line notes

- L01: 'l~l': null between 'qu'il' and 'l'a'. The ◆-like first glyph of 'car' is read ∞ (C). 'arsoir' (# = A) = hier soir.
- L02: R 8 read as 'en' (entendre).
- L04: 'monsieur' = # ♀ 8 ◇ 4 R n r.
- L06: 'estoidt': a stray 4/+ before σ.
- L07: Initial '# r C 7 4 R' unresolved (marchie? archie?). Ends with the Δ-null, Lo with a superscript, and the ')' null.

## Clear close (f. 47v, below the cipher)

> Monseigneur, je me recommande [bien] humblement a vostre bonne grace, et prie Dieu vous donner [?en bonne] santé longue vie. A Romme le xi^me jour d'octobre.
>
> Vostre humble serviteur,
> G. de Gramont, E[vesque] de Tarbe

The word before 'santé' is cramped; 'en bonne' is a guess. 'bien' is not clearly written. In the subscription 'Vostre' is abbreviated 'Vre'.

A ⊞ sign, the same one used in f. 47r L27, stands in the left margin near the signature (x ~ 0.23, y ~ 0.66 of the image). It may be a filing/cross-reference mark or show-through from the other side; I could not tell which.

## Continuous reading (modernised)

Et je crois qu'il l'a su, car hier soir il me fit entendre comment il avait commencé à faire le malade en attendant des nouvelles de monsieur l'amiral, et que si, au départ de cette ville, il était en bonne volonté, il était à cette heure en [?] bonne volonté.

## English summary

The 7 cipher lines go on from f. 47r (the subject is probably the Pope). "And I think he knew it, for last evening he told me how he had begun to feign illness while waiting for news from the admiral. He also said that if he was well disposed when he left this city, he was now [even more?] well disposed." Then the clear close: compliments, dated Rome, 11 October, signed G. de Gramont, bishop of Tarbes.

Confidence: 6 of 7 lines read securely. The first word of L7 ('# r C 7 4 R') does not resolve. Note: there are 7 cipher lines here, not 9.
