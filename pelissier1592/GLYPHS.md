## Additions from the pass-3 page readings of no. 22

- **τ** (a single hooked tau with a curl, without the "1"): **g** throughout no. 22 (grand, recogneurent, longuement,
  siege, religion, Champaigne, campaigne, soulager, negliger, cognoistre). Tokenised `12`. Earlier passes had it as T, z, tt, 7.
- **ƽ** (5-shaped sign): usually **n** (espannole, estrange, Bourbon; token `s`), but **g** in "langaiges" (gloss-confirmed),
  "tesmoignage". `5g` (5 with a bar) = g (garnison, iuger).
- **∝** (alpha-shaped): **g** (soulagement, logis). Tokenised `12`.
- **Xb**: the cross with a vertical tail (or bar) is **b** (foiblesse, obiecter, doubter, Pais bas, habitent, semblable).
- **7 / y**: b or h by context (b in bien, foiblesse, nombre; h in histoires, bouche, catholic, cheval, changer).
- **3**: b (Bourbon, bonnes, un bon), ss (necessaire), once g (guerre).
- **Looped ℓ with a flat foot**: u (villes, aduenu, pourueu, reunir) or e (esleué, necessité, iusques) by context.
- **Small box II** (x-height, flat bars): **e** (l'armée, composée, nées) — distinct from the tall ∐ = l.
- **S** (tall ∫∫) is **rr** in pourroit, Angleterre, guerre; single tall ʃ with a curled foot = rr.
- **Barred Ƶ / Ƒ**: q, z, s, n, and **o** (discours, nation, contentement, couvert, non, Chalons).
- **A** is n four times on f. 48v and in moyens/plaintes (f. 50r), against the calibration; scribe variation.
- **The small cross `t`** is e almost everywhere on f. 50r; `+` = h only in a few places.
- **∞** (joined ꝏ) is et in some places (gloss-confirmed at f. 46r C30/31); the separate `oo` is m.
- **Code groups**: 315 = the Béarnais (confirmed again: "appeler [315] (comme par miracle) a la succession");
  310 = probably the Duke of Savoy ("ce que pretend [310]", with an ambassador at court and a link to the governor of Milan;
  one occurrence, context only); 441 = probably France ("non seulement en [441] mais en toutes aultres regions de la
  Chrestiente"; one occurrence).
