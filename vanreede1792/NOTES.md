# Van Reede to William V, Berlin 4 Feb 1792 (DECODE R1057)

Status: read (2026-09-21).

Target: catalogue 217, KHA The Hague, Prins Willem V inv.nr. 205; DECODE R1057, one page,
numerical nomenclator, "partially decrypted" (an interlinear gloss over the first eight lines).
Prior art: DECODE carries the transcription (XZ, 2020) and a garbled reading of the gloss
("monsieur de Raney eal avenant... blanc he de ci r cle..."); nobody had read the
letter through, and the tail of 19 groups was never glossed. No printed edition found.

## Method

Same key as vanreede1787: the 1782 "Grand Chiffre" of William V, DECODE R1024
(`../vanreede1787/decode_grand_chiffre.py`, `decode/DOC_R1057_D1939_1939.txt`). Checked
against the image `../vanreede1787/decode/IMG_R1057_I5543_P1.png`. Transcription corrected
in one place: 4204 is 4202 (*tro*, which the gloss confirms). No solving was needed; the key
decodes every glossed group straight through.

> Monsieur de Larrey est averti qu'il y a du ci-tro-n sur les quatre demi-pages blanches
> de l'in-clu-se. Il voudra bien en prévenir Monseigneur le Prince d'Orange et avoir la bonté
> de lui remettre ma lettre, que je n'adresse point pour ne pas se[r?]… Les chiffres suivants
> sont des non-valeurs.

"M. de Larrey is warned that there is lemon juice [invisible ink] on the four blank
half-pages of the enclosure. He will kindly tell His Highness the Prince of Orange and be so
good as to hand him my letter, which I do not address [to him directly] so as not to [draw
notice]. The following figures are nulls."

The rest (3633 3160 370 1229 3645 50 3139 3650 2768 2800 3370 1681 559 57 3268 2775 1391
1268 2239 2560 57) are, by the letter's own statement, nulls; decoded they give a jumble
(Conseil d'Etat, la Russie, Archiduc, reconnoître) planted to occupy a reader. Larrey is
Thomas Isaac de Larrey, the Prince's secretary.

So the cipher hides no political content: it is a cover note telling the secretary the
real despatch is written in lemon juice on the blank half-pages of the enclosure. That
enclosure is not on the DECODE record.

Open: "pour ne pas 186 4115" (pais-ser) — probably *passer*/*paraître*, wording of
one group uncertain.
