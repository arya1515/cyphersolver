# Edward Lee (and Ghinucci) to Wolsey from Spain, 1526-1529 (BL Cotton MS Vespasian C III-C IV; DECODE R8458-R8587, 17 records)

Status: no write-up

Catalogue no. 103 (DECODE refill, rule-scored class B, "Ed. Lee (Hispania) to unknown recipient, 17 ciphertexts").
Worked 21 Sept 2026.

Outcome: **read at the time; closed.** Nothing new to read.

## What the records are

All 46 images of the 17 records were downloaded (DECODE cookie) and every page was looked at.

- The "unknown recipient" is Wolsey: every letter opens "Please it your grace" and speaks of "the King's highness"
  and "my lord legate". From 1527 the letters are joint despatches of Edward Lee (King's almoner) and Girolamo
  Ghinucci, bishop of Worcester, signed "Hier. Eps Wigorniensis / Edouarde Lee". Sevilla ("Civill") March-April 1526,
  Valladolid May-July 1527, Valladolid Feb 1529 (1528 old style).
- **16 of the 17 records contain no cipher at all.** R8458, R8459, R8460, R8462 (Vesp. C III ff. 227-232, 1526) and
  R8536, R8537, R8538, R8542, R8543, R8544, R8549, R8550, R8561, R8585, R8586, R8587 (Vesp. C IV, 1527-29) are clear
  English despatches with addresses and dockets. DECODE's "Non-decrypted" status on them is wrong.
- **R8566** (Vesp. C IV ff. 170-173 new foliation 178-181, Valladolid 17 July 1527, Lee and Ghinucci) is the only one
  with cipher: four short runs in the Lee-Worcester sign cipher (images 2, 4 and 5), each with a contemporary
  decipherment written above it. It is the only record with DECODE status "Partially decrypted"; that status is this
  interlinear decipherment. The postscript says the other matters are "in cyphre".

The interlinear glosses, as legible on the images, are in plaintext.txt (100 words). Among them: Lee and
Ghinucci propose "the doughter of portyngale for the duk of rychemont" (Henry Fitzroy) and discuss giving the
duchy of Milan back to Francesco Sforza.

Check against the key: S. Tomokiyo's reconstruction of the Lee-Worcester cipher (1526-1529) is attached to R8566
as DOC_8566_2024-Oct-10-20-38-38_40106.png. Under the gloss "possibilite ... good", the signs
`† ⊥ & - ζ - 9 - ʒ v` and `Δ ⊥ ⊥ δ` read p-o-ss-i-b-i-l-i-t-e and g-o-o-d with his table.

Prior art: read at the time; Tomokiyo published the key (cryptiana); the letters are calendared in Letters and
Papers Henry VIII vol. IV. The sister record R8567 (Lee and Poyntz, July 1527) was closed the same way (poyntz1527/).
