## Coverage

**Every cipher line on the leaf is read.** Bands 1 and 2 of the deskewed strips are not cipher: band 1 is the
top margin carrying the folio number *162*, band 2 the docket *11 de Março 93* with show-through above it.
The cipher proper runs from band 3 to band 16, and all fourteen carry readings. What remains open is the
groups marked `·` within them.

## Sense

Sessa writes to Ibarra from Rome about **the testimonies that they raise against everyone**; the legate; the
duke of Feria; **those of us who live in Rome**; a matter that **does not last four days**; what **passes to
the successors**, and how **ordinary** these things have become; **difficulties in the election**, and with
all that; the business is **so important** that **he hopes he will favour it**; **in such a manner that it
suits**, among those who follow; **diverse pretensions**, in each one, **if I am not mistaken**; and
**I cannot refrain from doing it, at least, so that the ministers are not in agreement** — **won over** —
**of which they cannot be trusted**.

The nomenclature groups carry the names and the political vocabulary: *vos* = **Roma**, *rol* = **election**,
*nor* = **negocios**, *cer* = **manera**, *ʃes* = **causa**, *ʃur* = **ministro**, *far* = **mente**,
*vom* = **legato**, *vas* = **seignor**. `4̄`, with the bar, is the numeral **4** itself.
