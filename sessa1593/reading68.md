# fr. 3984 no. 68, f. 145 (canvas 268) — Don Diego de Ibarra, Paris, 10 July 1593

**A different cipher from the other three.** This leaf uses the *Cipher between the Count of Mansfeld and the
Duke of Feria (1593)*, which Tomokiyo published as `spanish3mansfeld.png` and cites at fr. 3984 ff. 130 and
145. Its numbering is unrelated to the 1592-93 cipher: here ba = 24, ca = 25, da = 10, la = 59, ma = 49,
na = 40, pa = 79, ra = 99, sa = 74, ta = 90, va = 84, ya = 85, que = 66, and *de* is the single figure 8.
Letters: a = x / 2, e = p / 3, o = r, u = n, y = ∂. Marks: `~` doubles the consonant, `+` final -l,
`v` final -n, `.` final -r, `°` final -s, bar = the number itself. Transcribed to `keyM.tsv`; `decodeM.py`
applies it.

The letter opens **in clear** and drops into cipher mid-sentence:

> *&ldquo;… a la venida del mensagero que despache, porque espero que me dara V.S.a mas claridad de las
> cossas, porque en la primera me dise V.S.a …&rdquo;*

The three-letter groups — *pra, rom, mon, xen, vul, cil, denp, hes, dur* — are this cipher's nomenclature,
which Tomokiyo notes in passing (*vas* for *segnor*) but does not tabulate; they are not yet resolved.

## Sense

Ibarra writes from Paris on 10 July 1593, at the height of the Estates, about **the coming of the messenger
he is despatching**, hoping for **more clarity on matters**; **cavalry being awaited**; something that **does
not continue**; and **that he may have received his** …
