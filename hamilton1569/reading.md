# Mary Queen of Scots to John Hamilton, Archbishop of St Andrews, Bolton, 18 January 1569 — the contemporary decipherment

BL Add MS 33531 ff. 73–74 (DECODE R8347). ff. 73r–73v are Mary's letter: a clear opening and close in Scots with
the body in cipher. ff. 74r–74v are a contemporary decipherment of the cipher body on a separate sheet, in Scots, in a
secretary hand. Transcribed here 2026-09-21 line by line from the full-resolution DECODE images (P1 = f. 74r,
P3 = f. 74v, P2 = f. 73r, P4 = f. 73v).

Conventions: line breaks as on the leaf; `[?]` a word read with doubt; `[…]` illegible; `⟨sign⟩` a code sign that
the decipherer copied through without resolving (see NOTES, "Signs left in the decipherment"); ~~struck~~ words
deleted by the decipherer; `^word^` an interlineation.

## f. 73v, clear close (Mary's hand)

> As concerning the commissioun ze desire us to send to the erlis of Argyle, Eglingtoun, Cassillis and Boyd, we
> knaw not in quhat maner ze desire the same, for we understand to haif alreddy
> ane generall Lieutenent. Allwayis quhat commissioun that ze think salbe necessair
> wt ze counsell advyse thair upon, cause mak it in sic forme as maye be profitable,
> and send us the same quhilk we sall subscryve ^and put ony day the dait^ [cipher, glossed "als sall be necessarie"]
> And gif it be neidfull to mak sic commissioun
> to ane Lieutenent, as he may constitute ony of the saidis lordis deputis for him
> in his absence, Send us the same in forme and we sall subscryve it. Sua
> Committis zow to the protectioun of god almychtie. Off Bowtoun the
> [xviij] of Januar 1568.
>
> Zour gud Cusines and asuryd frinde
> Marie R

The day in the date line is only partly legible on the image; Bain reads 18 January.
