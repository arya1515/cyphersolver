# Key brief: Ferdinand I to Malvezzi, 23 Jan 1548 (DECODE R366)

Latin letter, clear text with ciphered passages. Monoalphabetic homophonic cipher with graphic signs, key
DECODE R367 (images img/k367_1s.jpg, img/k367_2s.jpg; code list img/k367_3s.jpg/k367_4s.jpg if made) and the
Hungarian archive sheet img/k386s.jpg.

## Letters
- a: M, = (two bars), # (hash)
- b: ( and ), also Latin c
- c: +, ‡ (cross with two bars)
- d: § / 8-shape, ⊙ (dotted circle)
- e/ae: K (Latin k), a circle on a stem (short, like ϙ; sometimes with a second small circle), #o (hash followed by small o)
- f: v, ^
- g: π-with-feet / Ш-like (a Π with a base bar)
- h: λ, ʎ
- i: Γ, 7, o7 (small o before 7)
- l: ⌣ and ⌢ (breve and inverted breve, ∩)
- m: F, f
- n: P, b (also a hooked b that looks like h)
- o: ∂ (reversed 6), °P (P with small circle to the left), stacked 8-with-P shapes
- p: T, ⊥
- q: ♉ (taurus), α
- r: Latin e, ʛ (g-like with a tail)
- s: Latin a, Π (capital pi, "π" with a long top bar)
- t: ♁ (circle with a cross above), ♀ (circle with a cross below)
- u/v: circle on a stem (ϙ), -o, o- (small o with a tail left or right)
- x: 8/&/9;  y: □;  et: ∇ Δ
- doubles: 3=cc 4=dd 5=ff 6=gg 7=ll 8=mm 12=tt 13=ss 14=rr 15=pp 16=nn; 50=bellum 51=pax
- dies: N-like sign, mensis: ɕ, annus: Ш with three bars

The circle-on-stem family is ambiguous (u, e/ae, and with a cross t). Resolve by Latin context.

## Nulls
Latin-looking whole words inside the cipher are NULLS: etiam, idcirco, atque/atq, cum, Dac, Dar, Dec, Der, Porro,
spo, spu, and the loose letters b, d, n, z, π, Z. Skip them.

## Code words (Latin-looking syllables)
ki = Rustanus (Rüstem Pasha); ka / ke = Passa; le = S.M. Caesarea (Charles V); li = Venetia; Mis = Justus de Argento;
Ab = Galli; Bi = Rex Galliae; Cad = Constantinopolis; Eb = orator; Nas / Ras / ye = Imperator Turcorum;
Nis = Junus Bey; Ros = Persae; Sis = Ibrahim Passa; Vb = rex Poloniae; zes = Regina Isabella; zis = Joannes Sigismundus;
zos = Frater Georgius (Martinuzzi); zi = Hungaria; zus = Hungariae imperium.
