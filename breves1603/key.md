# Savary de Brèves cipher, 1602–1603 — original key

Source: BnF fr. 3462 f. 103 (Gallica ark btv1b9058227h, canvas 109; full-res 7541×4902 in `src/fr3462_c109_full.jpg`,
tiles in `key/`). Tomokiyo reconstructed this key from Brèves' letters in fr. 16144 and later noticed it
(henryiv.htm, "[f.103] [p.111] Savary de Breves ... the same as Savary de Breves' Cipher (1602-1603)").
The neighbouring leaf f. 104 (canvas 110) is the *different* "Chiffre reformé pour Levant" sent to Brèves in April 1604.

Glyph names below are descriptive (the alphabet is written with digits, pseudo-letters and signs). Numeric
nomenclator codes are read directly; symbol codes are described.
