# Smith cipher passages, BL Add MS 4136 (Forbes copy): decipherment B

Partial first pass. Readings come from the glyphs through KEY.md and the key image R9261 p.1. Cribs were used only to
check them. `[?]` means an uncertain letter or word, and `⟨code⟩` means an unread code group. Letters marked
NOT ATTEMPTED have no reading yet.

## Smith to Cecil, Blois 7 Mar 1562/3: R9252 p.1 (lower half) and p.2 top (Forbes f.163-164)
Crib: Forbes, *Full View* vol. 2, pp. 350-353 (forbes2.txt l.29152-29406). The ciphered passages fall in the
paragraph "Ye know now what is to be done ... will not lenger be dalied withall" (p. 350).

- (1) `Hull ⌐5:L2 1 T̈ hastings M̈ {π910L / ⊤:πξL` → *[Hull null]* **even** ⟨1⟩ ⟨T̈⟩ *[null]* ⟨M̈ = matter?⟩ ⟨{π910L⟩ **t i [c?] l e**.
  Only partly read. "even" is sure (⌐=e, 5:=v, L=e, 2=n). ⊤:πξL may be "tickle" (Forbes has "so ticlish"), but ξ is a d-form in the key, so the reading is left open.
- (2) `∝γ T̈ b: and` → not read (⟨∝γ⟩ ⟨T̈⟩ be *[and null]*).
- (3) `Jersey Ẅ 7 6: 2: ∝ω⊔7·ʒL garnesey ⊢L4: ⊕ :k` → *[null]* **without charge** *[null]* **let [Admiral] know**.
  Matches Forbes "done without charge. Let the admirall know". ʒ as g is uncertain.
- (4) `Dunkirk ·h A: ⊕A· and C̈ 8·5⊢L6·` → *[null]* **have all** ⟨⊕A· = Normandy?⟩ *[and null]* ⟨C̈ = again/this?⟩ **somer**.
  Matches Forbes "that ye will have all Normandy again this somer". The ⊕A· sign is not in the key's name list as read.
- (5) `London ·1 f: T·⊢ byrause 9{π π2}10: ·1 :k` → *[null]* **I** ⟨f:⟩ ⟨T·⊢ = tell?⟩ *[null]* **plainly I know**.
  Matches "I tell them plainly, I know".
- (6) `C: ω⊔5:L` → ⟨C: = they?⟩ **have**. Matches "what captaines they have".
- (7) `C:: ⊓1 ⊓‡{L T̈ m:` → **they** [?] **a[r?] able to make**. Matches "what men ... they are able to make".
- (8) `♀-box` → **the Queen's Majesty**.
- (9) `Dunkirk f ω5ſfu⊔XL10· 76·⊔96:7·π2∝L9· f ⊖ ʕ1⊓ T̈ b: / 7·L2{6·π{:.. / 2·5:πL2:}9: G. L̈ bycause` →
  **for** ⟨...⟩ **... for [England?] ... be ...**. Not read. The crib is "now the weather doth com in propyce for it, will not lenger be dalied withall", but I could not align the glyphs to it without forcing them.
- (10) `ñ ⁚M T̈ θL9⌐ ⊸` → not read (⊸ = Newhaven sign?).
- (11) `Lyn a· C: θL95 ⊙— F: 7:10· bycause` → *[null]* ... ⟨θL9⌐ = keep?⟩ **Calais** **from** ⟨7:10·⟩. Probably the crib's "to kepe Calais from us" (p. 351). "keep" is doubtful (θ=k, L=e, 9=p gives "kep").
- (12) `Jersey T̈ ω⊓5:L ⊙— A·` → *[null]* ⟨T̈⟩ **have Calais again**. Not confirmed.
- (13) `Boston ∝52□L⊓π{ T·6·7:L⊓θ3410· ·A / T7· 2Lξ·o·∞ □6·L2ξ10·ωπ98⌐ Dover C: ‡L∝π2 35o·o / T̈ 8·L⊔4 C6:M garnesey` → **...friends...** ⟨mostly unread⟩. The fragment □6·L2ξ10· reads "frends" (friendship?). Crib: "they have ... confessid ... ther nede of our frendshippe".
- (14) `·h obiM̈f·1 C8·ʕ·A T̈ ʕ London` → not read.
- (15) `Hull ·o ⊳` → **of [Queen of Scots]**. Matches "the entermeadling of the queen of Scots".
- (16) `♀-box π10` → **the Queen's Majesty** ⟨π10⟩.

## Summary
- Attempted: 7 Mar 1562/3 (16 passages) and 1 Sep 1564 (31 passages, mostly single name symbols).
- Read securely or in large part: 7 Mar (3), (4), (5), (6), (7), (8), (15), and much of (16); 1 Sep (9), (12), and the
  name symbols.
- Uncertain or unread: 7 Mar (1), (2), (9)-(14); most of 1 Sep.
- Agreement with Forbes: every passage read in 7 Mar matches Forbes p. 350-351 in wording and order, without any
  change to the key.
- Key observations: in this hand 5· is r and 6· is r or s; ⌐ is e; ⊢ serves as l in "let"; the dotted Latin letters
  (C:, C̈, T̈) need a closer look at the word column of the key.
- Remaining: all letters after 1 Sep 1564 (R9252 p.2 bottom and p.3, R9253, R9254 p.1-2).
