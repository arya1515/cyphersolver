## 1. 15 Oct 1562 (y ~380-770)
Forbes: "I am certaynlie advertised by diverse Scots, that there be divers sent allredie ... to move the queen of
Scots to break with you, and to make war out of hand; and that the mater is now in hand ... that the hostages
which you have shall be convoyed of into Flanders ... secretary Rollet and monsieur Vyllemort."

## 3. 15/17 Dec 1562 (pasted slip, y ~1770-2500)
Forbes (15 Dec): "Methincks, there is no good to be done that way for Calais ... I se, you must hold ferme and
fast: Frenchmen will com to reason, but compellid. Ye must give marvelous warning to My Lorde of Warwick to
trust no Frenchman, of what religion soever he be; and that he take marvelous hid of treason, for in that is all
their trust: for whither thei fight with the prince of Conde, which I do not beleve, or they agree within
themselfes, which is most to be suspectid; as sone as ever they be able, they will thither will all their force."

  "[Newhaven] is not so strong as supposed; if it were summer they would attempt to afray our men thence with a
  garden [(2) Xπ6ξL3 = garden, 9πξL = side] of chamillion [(3), first read 'Chatillon'] upon the hill on this side ...

  pretence has given him letters to the Lord Robert and Mr. Secretary, to whom he wrote from Rouen of the same matter.

  most necessary." The unread group "dis?che" = la Rochelle. Read in full.
