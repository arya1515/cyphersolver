# Smith cipher passages, BL Add MS 4136: decipherment C

## Smith to Cecil, Avignon, 4 Oct 1564 (Forbes heading read so far as "27 Oct"): R9252 p.2 foot and p.3, Add MS 4136 ff.164-165

**Date.** The heading reads "from Avignon, 4 Oct. 1564". The figure is the same looped 4 as the 4 in "1564"; it is not "27".
CSP Foreign vol. 7 has no Smith letter of 27 Oct. It does have **no. 714, Smith to Cecil, Avignon, 1 Oct. 1564, P.S.
4 Oct.** ("Orig., portions in cipher, deciphered"; pp. 217-218). Every firm reading below follows no. 714 §2 in
order, so this is that letter. Crib saved in `csp/v7_pp215-233.html`.

Transcription conventions follow decipher_A.md. Clear nulls (Hull, Calais, Sandwich, Jersey, Antwerpe, bycause) are
dropped. Glyph notes for this letter:
- `ϕ` (a looped kite with a stem) = w (2:ϕ6 = two, three times).
- `{{` / `ſſ` / `ꟿ` = st (papistry, state, stronger, strong).
- `ʋ` (a small open v) = l (litle, gospel, Lenox).
- `ζ` variants (a T with a hook): `ζ` + dot = here (1, 24, 31); `ζ̤` = his/they/them (see each row); `ζ̈` = they/this.
  These dot placements are not stable in Forbes's copy, so they are read from context. Such rows are marked [?].
- `J̣` = is/are; `!` = in/into; `凸`-variant = Earl (before Murray, Lennox, Warwick; plain 凸 = England in 14).
- `B:` = Bishop; `C̄` = Congregation; `M̅` = marriage (33, 41, 48); `ṃ` = marry; `Ṗ` privy, `.P` practise.

## Summary
- Letter identified: the heading's date is **4 Oct 1564**, not 27 Oct; it is CSP Foreign vii **no. 714** (Avignon,
  1 Oct with P.S. 4 Oct 1564, "portions in cipher, deciphered"). The ciphered passages carry §2 of the calendar
  entry in order, from "Their designs here are two" to "this last offer of Lennox's".
- 62 numbered passages (there is no (6)), plus an unnumbered line (12) and a run-on (29b).
  - About 50 read firmly: 1-4, 7, 9-11, 13-16, 19-21, 24-29b, 31-33, 35-38, 40-51, 53-57, 59-63.
  - About 10 read in part or uncertain: 5, 8, 12, 17, 18, 22, 23, 30, 34, 39, 52, 58.
  - 22 and 23 ("by little and little and by … great") have no counterpart in the CSP summary and are only partly read.
- Code words used here beyond decipher_A: ϕ = w; ſſ/ꟿ = st; ʋ = l; J̣ = is/are; ! = in/into. The ζ family
  (here/his/they/this/thing) and the Earl sign carry the most doubt, because Forbes's dots are not consistent.
- The crib was used only to check readings. The spelled-out words (designs, papistry, gospel, Italian, contreband,
  interrupt, Glasgow, embassador, profession, Poictiers, bushoprick, reserved, dispatcheth, espials, negligent,
  anker, Robert, cardinals, Guise, stronger, Babtista, London, dispache, enclined, Lenox) come from the glyphs alone.
