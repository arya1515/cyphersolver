== GLYPH LEGEND
[a+]  'a' followed/crossed by a '+' / 't'-like stroke (looks like "at" or "a4"); single sign
4     plain '4'-shaped sign (also looks like crossed 't'/'+'); distinct from digit-pairs; standalone token
[tt]  'tt' / '++' double-cross sign; [ttt] triple-cross version ('ttt'/'#'-like)
u[tt], a[tt], f[tt], 2[tt], g[tt]  letter with following tt-stroke ligature (kept as one token)
[ft]  'f' with cross-bar (ft ligature); [ff] double f
[9]   '9'/'g'-like descender sign, standalone (looks like 'ep'/'9'); n[9], a[9], m[9], u[9], e[9] = letter + that tail (one sign, likely "no/ap/mo" style ligature)
[gt]  g with a t-cross tail; [gp] g with p-like tail (reads 'gp'); plain g = g with simple tail
[lg]  'l' joined to 'g' (looks like "lg"/"bg"); [lg]p / [lg]t variants
[ct]  'c' or 'l' joined to '+'/t (looks like "C+"/"Lt")
[eo]  'e o' ligature ('eo'/'to' looking)
[ss]  long-s pair ('ss'/'fs') p10
[x]   saltire 'x'-like cross, only on pp.16-17
[st]  s with t-cross (p17)
w     'w'/'co'-like sign (only pp.16-17)
v     'v' shaped sign (pp.16-17)
Numbers: first digit '5' is written like 'h'/'5' (50-59); '3' is written like 'z' (32,33,34,35); I read z+z-shape as 33, z+s-shape as 35 inconsistently (uncertain). '1' may be a dot/i separator rather than a number.
Two keys: pp.10-14 (letter part 1) vs pp.16-17 (signs x, w, v, 8, 20, 22, 78, 87 appear) -- likely a different table, or same table with a different scribe.
