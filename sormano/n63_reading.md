# No. 63 — Sormano and de Vaulx to François I, Ferrara, shortly after 23 February 1529

BnF fr. 3096 no. 63, ff. 115r–116v (Gallica `btv1b9060015d`, views 119–121).
The follow-up to the duplicate pair nos. 65/66: it opens *"Il xxIII del
presente per nostre duplicate lettere a vostra Maestà al lungo scrivessimo ciò
che havevamo negociato con questo Signor Duca"*. It is the longest and most
heavily enciphered of the three, and it has no sibling copy. It breaks off
mid-sentence at the foot of f. 116v; its end and date remain to be found.

Clear text as written; *italics* = deciphered; 《…》 = a ciphered stretch that
resists a continuous reading (the glyph string is in `n63_transcription.md`).
◎ = the DUCA nomenclator. Deciphered words are given only where the Italian is
secure; this leaf yields far less than nos. 65/66 because so much of it is cipher
and the segmentation errors compound over long runs.

## Summary (English)

1. Written a few days after the 23 February pair, it reports the sequel of the
   agents' audiences with Alfonso d'Este.
2. The duke had already given "a not very clear and not resolute answer" on the
   captaincy (*capitaneato*) of the French army for the Naples enterprise; he has
   not accepted it, and the agents doubt he will.
3. Alfonso says he cannot see that the King and his confederates are making the
   provisions the enterprise needs — an army in the field, supplies, money for
   Swiss, Spaniards and Genoese — and that everything should be furnished before
   he commits.
4. Two things he asks the King to remember: the Naples enterprise, and (a
   second point around the captaincy) that he cannot take the command without
   the King's explicit provision.
5. He promises, from his side, not to fail; the agents say they have omitted
   nothing that was in their power.
6. Given the duke's nature, they judge the matter "not a little difficult", and
   put their hope in the authority and credit of the marshal Teodoro Trivulzio,
   expected in Ferrara on his way to Venice; de Vaulx will wait three or four
   days more for him.
7. The letter breaks off; its final leaf is not at f. 117.
