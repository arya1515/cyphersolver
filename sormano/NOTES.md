# Sormano and de Vaulx to François I, Ferrara, February 1529

BnF français 3096, nos. 63, 65 and 66 (Gallica ark
`btv1b9060015d`). These letters concern Ferrara's defection from France before
the Peace of Cambrai. No continuous transcription or decipherment of the three
target letters has yet been completed.

The BnF catalogue sequence and visible foliation give f. 115 for no. 63. A
secondary Cryptiana/Lasry reference calls it f. 105; that appears to be a folio
error rather than a second copy.

## Cipher and key

The pages use one continuous homophonic substitution alphabet, with special
signs for doubled `cc` and `ll` and no transposition identified. George Lasry
reconstructed the key in 2023; Tomokiyo published the table on Cryptiana. The
local transcription in `KEY.md` was checked on no. 62: its first crib begins
`de vn capitaneo suizari ...`. `key_lasry3.png` is the verified copy of the
published table, and `decode.py` applies ASCII aliases for hand transcription.

The common hand and symbol repertoire make nos. 62 and 67 suitable cribs for
all three targets, but possession of the alphabet is not a reading: the mixed
clear/cipher lines still require glyph-by-glyph transcription.

## Files and next step

`AGENT_BRIEF.md` records the IIIF crop route, glyph distinctions, Italian
reading conventions and required output format. The intended deliverables are
`n63_transcription.md` / `n63_reading.md`, and corresponding pairs for nos. 65
and 66. None of those six files exists yet. The next step is to transcribe no.
65 and no. 66 in parallel, align the duplicate passages to resolve ambiguous
glyphs, then read no. 63 against the shared key and the two deciphered controls.
Raw Gallica pages and exploratory crops are kept locally and are not tracked.
