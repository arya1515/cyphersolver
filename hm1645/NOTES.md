Status: no write-up

**Verdict: already READ, by George Lasry (DECODE uploads dated 2020-10-23).** Catalogue entry 59 (DECODE R785, R787,
R790, R923, R924, R929) and its siblings, entries 54 (R752), 55 (R928) and 60 (R789), are all covered. Nothing about

## What DECODE holds

Each record's page links three or four text files beside the image (fetched with the shared cookie into `decode/`,
git-ignored because they are DECODE's and Lasry's work):

- a manual ciphertext transcription by Sofia Pettersson ("SofPe"), July–Oct 2019;
- a **reconstructed key** by George Lasry, 2020-10-23, one per key period: "TNA SP 106" (Sept 1645, homophonic,
  no nomenclator numbers below 100, words 124+), "SP106-2" (July–Aug 1645, homophonic with nomenclator),
  "SP106-5" (Jan 1646, homophonic, nomenclator of letter+digit groups like n3, k1), "SP106-8" (Jan 1646, homophonic
  with a numbered word list 100+);
- Lasry's **plaintext** of every letter in the run, ff. 207–247 (0207-0210, 0211-0212, 0213-0215, 0216-0217,
  0218-0221, 0219, 0223-0228, 0230-0232, 0233-0234, 0235-0236, 0238-0240, 0245-0246, 0247), with dating notes.

DECODE's status "Partially decrypted" reflects the nomenclator numbers still unresolved (`<425>`, `<241>`, `<199>`,

## Check done here

Applied Lasry's SP106-5 alphabet to Pettersson's raw transcription of f. 247 (R929), independently of his plaintext
file. It reads at once: "…ARIS … IAN … SR … RECEIVED <425>S … DECEMB … IANVARY … <199>S <429> THANKES … CONTINUATI[ON]
… <425> C[A]RE … WEKLY ADVERTISEMENTS … STATE … AFFAIRES HOW BAD … EVER … DESPERATE … ABLE … SUBS[IS]T … WITHOUT
FARTHER LOSSES … TILL OUR ENDEAVOURS … RELEIF … BROUGHT … EFFECT … LIKEWISE CONCERNING THE EMBASSAGE AND
MARRIAGE", matching his plaintext. The letter+digit groups (n3 = the, k1 = of, ...) are nomenclator.
