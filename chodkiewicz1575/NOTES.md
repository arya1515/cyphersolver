## Prior solution

Nils Kopal and Michelle Waldispühl, "Two Encrypted Diplomatic Letters Sent by Jan Chodkiewicz to Emperor
Maximilian II in 1574-1575", *HistoCrypt 2023* (Linköping Electronic Conference Proceedings),
https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/view/160/116.

- This record is their **letter E**: "ABSCHRIFT IOHANN CHODTKIEUIZ SCHREIPENS AN DIE MAJESTÄT", a copy, sent from
  Szkłów (Shklow, Belarus), 22 February 1575; HHStA Chiffrenschlüssel fasc. 20, kt 210-212. Their letter D is the
  sibling ff. 205-208, Vilnius, 15 November 1574.
- Cipher: homophonic substitution of graphic signs, German with short Latin passages, day numbers and year in plain
  digits. Not the key of Maximilian's own 1575 letters (their A-C). Key rebuilt with the CrypTool 2 Homophonic
  Substitution Analyzer; "We were able to decipher both letters (D and E) completely."
- The German plaintext is on the DECODE record (the paper says the plaintexts of all five letters are there).
- Content (their summary): salutation; receipt of Maximilian's message; loyalty; doubts that the emperor's plan
  would bring "all sorts of repulsive thoughts ... confusions and splits"; proposes sending his servant Adam Theim to
  win the Lithuanian electorate, then Polish nobles, for the Habsburg candidacy; advises a contract first with the
  higher nobility only; closes with an apology for a suggestion that might annoy the emperor.
- Open problem they name: the letter is filed as a copy, so an original may survive elsewhere in the HHStA.

DECODE's "Partially decrypted" status understates it; queued a correction to "Decrypted".
