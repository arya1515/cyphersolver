# Colbert 390, Bourdin-countersigned letters of 1561 with margin decipherments (calibration for c392 p.231)

Source: BnF 500 Colbert 390, Gallica btv1b10033942k. Crops in `c390b/` (b34_* = 1600x330 native bands of view 34
right page, m34_* = margin gloss).

How the gloss works: the decipherment is written as a continuous text in the left margin, starting level with the
first cipher line. It is not line-for-line: the cipher lines hold more text than the margin lines, so the gloss
falls behind (by about 7 lines at the foot of p.67). Alignment is therefore done as a running text. The cipher is
the same system as c392 p.231 (La, xy, q̄q, ℌ, Ĝ, W, ‖, ¢, Ʒ, ſ, 6, 9, io, <, #, at, π).

## Result for the c392 gaps

- G (Ĝ) = t: confirmed (already t in c392_p231n.txt).
- at = par, π = h, k = i: confirmed; they were already in the token file.
- £/Ls = dit: confirmed ("dudit"), so c392 l.7 "si le[dit] … Roy" is now P on an exemplar.
- 7 = nous on p.67 (secure), but "vous" fits p.68: the sign is a pronoun sign, nous by default.
- ² in 1561 is a raised hooked 5/s shape = r. The c392 "²" is a flat small 2 and looks like a z variant (a),
  so the 1561 value is not transferred.
- Not found: maue, tt, ss (small), ꝫ, ll-underlined. πꝫ stays unread (π = h is secure, ꝫ unknown).

### View 26, rest of p.50 and p.51 (finished)

Further readings on p.50, with the same key:
- "3 d q̄q 7 b d 9 Ʒ c 9 ¢ ſ u c io ℌ ſ Ʒ ll u io θ" = "ce que **nous** pensons sçavoir acquis": 7 = nous, plain
  ll = q again.

…"). They give "La u Ĝ ℌ ſ 9 Ʒ b c ℌ Ĝ" = du transport and "‖ 6 ſ La u d 9 io ℌ" = et à l'advenir. No new signs.

None of maue, underlined ll, ss, tt, ꝫ or La b ſſ x occurs on pp.50-51.
