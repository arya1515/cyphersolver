# Bishop of Rennes' cipher (1561-1564) — working key
Source: Tomokiyo reconstruction (images/CharlesIX_Rennes.png), calibrated on the
glossed cribs fr. 3181 f. 52 (17 May 1563, La Ferrière II pp. 37-38) and f. 57
(13 Aug 1563, La Ferrière II p. 79-80 "A Monsieur l'évêque de Rennes").

Empirical confirmations (f.52/f.57 cribs):
- q̄q̄=que, at=par, <=vous, ıō=i, ſ=a, #=i/y (not just est!), λ=v, d=e, µ=u, 3=c, Ɔ=m
- "princes" = b ʒ m̨ 9 m ↘o ҳ̶ → p=b, r=ʒ(z-swash), i=m̨, n=9, c=m, e=↘o(?), s=ҳ̶
  CAUTION: ʒ(z-swash) serves r here though Tomokiyo lists it under s; and ↘o under e —
  scribe's shapes for r/s and e/u homophones need care token by token.
- la=Ⱡa ligature appears as 'La' in clear-looking script: it is cipher d... no — 'la' small
  in line = d homophone La-lig. Distinguish from mn-bar = word 'la'.
