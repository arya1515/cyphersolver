## Running text

Monsieur, je vous envoye la lettre que […] la Royne escript […] sur le faict dont vous m'avez donné advis.
Dernierement […] advisé que l'en ne luy parle de la secrete, qu'el[le …] vous, en moindres paroles et termes que
de luy dire, ne la […], qu'elle la trouve telle que de sa propre personne; de sorte que si le […] Roy n'y est
retenu en ce que l'on desire d'aultre occasion que celle-là, il n'y […] devera plus faire de difficulté. Au
demeurant […] vous advise que ce que je vous mande, que [on] sçavoit incontinant en Espaigne. Tous les pro[…]
que [luy?] teniez secret […] ne fut pas s[a]ns occasion, [et j']eus mes[me] advis que [s]a femme […] [a]voit [fait?] triumphe de la
premiere ouverture que vous luy feistes de [ce] mariage. Je croy bien que […] il y aura à peu changer d'advis
[et] conseil, me semble, que [on] en donne assez, com[me] en grande occasion. Dieu veuille que toutes
[choses] se conduise[nt] et succede[nt] au commun desir et contentement de [leurs] Majestés, avec un tel et
si ferme establissement d'amitié et alliance que […] nous tous en ressentions, tant d'une part que d'aultre, le
bien et l'utilité, à l'honneur de Dieu et [au] repos de la chrestienté.

## English summary

Bourdin sends the bishop the Queen's letter on the matter the bishop reported. The secret matter (the marriage)
is to be broached with the other side only in guarded words. The King should not be held back if no better
occasion than this one offers. Whatever is written to him is known at once in Spain, so the bishop must keep
it secret. Something that happened was "not without cause" and was a triumph for the first overture the bishop
made about the marriage. Bourdin expects little change of counsel. He closes: God grant everything goes as
[their] Majesties wish, with a firm bond of friendship and alliance, for the good of both sides, the honour of
God and the peace of Christendom.

### Final gaps and why they resist

- l.1 `maue`: a unique shape that matches nothing in the key image. It may be a name sign (the Queen Mother's
  addressee?).
- l.2 `ſ λ ²`: this occurrence of ² has no exemplar, so "au …" cannot be completed.
- l.3 `‡‡ ~`: here ‡‡ does not fit either nn or et ("et advisé" is ungrammatical). The tilde is unknown.
- l.4 `d z xy 9`: "qu'el[le] ?aen vous". None of the value combinations gives a word.
- l.5 `W xy u ʒ̊ d ℌ`: gives "ssevrer" or "bevrer". "sevrer" does not fit the sense.
- l.7 `z ss`: `ss` occurs only here and in l.20 (β ss Ʒ = l?s), so there is no exemplar.
- l.8-9 `ll ℌ z 9 tt`: "…rand". The underlined `ll` and `tt` occur nowhere else.
- l.10 `w xy`: w (↘o) occurs only here. Probably "je".
- l.11/17 `πꝫ`: occurs twice, and "on" fits both places. It is not in Tomokiyo's table.
- l.12 `La b ſſ x` and `b at`: "tous les pro[d p a z]". This may be a name or a copying slip.
- l.13 `G`, `ƒ 7`: isolated signs at the line join.
- l.14 `t7 =`: whether this is "y" or "a" is undecided.
- l.15 `ꝛ` before "mariage": p does not fit, so it may be the Z = "vous"/"ce" word sign.
- l.16 `La xy t7`: "de t…" before "il y aura".
- l.21 `x ‡‡`, l.22 `k est`: short connectors with no exemplar.

## Crib pass (cribs.md: La Ferrière I 448-451, 481-482, 503-505)

`colbert390_glossed.md` was not present when I checked, so it is not used here.

Context fixed by the cribs:
- "la lettre que … la Royne escript" is Catherine's letter of **15 Dec 1562** to Rennes (Colbert 390 f.115),
  countersigned by Bourdin. It deals with the Riffenberg / Archbishop of Trier overture for a marriage between
  Charles IX and a daughter of the King of the Romans.
- "[s]a femme … triumphe" fits the **Queen of the Romans, Maria, Philip II's sister**. That explains "sçavoit
  incontinant en Espaigne" and the need for secrecy.
- "si le … Roy n'y est retenu … d'aultre occasion" matches the crib's "retenues … que veult faire le roy
  d'Espaigne" and "encores que l'on die que c'est aux maris à rechercher les femmes". The cipher passage is about
  the King (Charles IX) not being held back from the match.

Gaps narrowed by the cribs (marked P-, meaning a probable reading resting on sense and a crib, not on sign
exemplars):
- l.1 `maue` → P- **Madame** ("la lettre que Madame la Royne escript"). The sign may be an abbreviation.
- l.2 `ſ λ ²` → P- **à vous** ("escript à vous sur le faict dont vous m'avez donné advis").
- l.10 `w xy` → P- **je** ("Au demeurant je vous advise"; the 18 Jan crib has the same formula).
- l.15 `ꝛ` → P- **ce** ("de ce mariage").
- l.7 `z ss` → still unread. The crib suggests "si le[dict] Roy" or a name sign, but no Riffenberg or Trier
  sign-string fits two signs.

Name signs: no occurrence of the Tomokiyo signs for l'Empereur (É-grave) or Roy de Bohême (V-dot) was found in
the transcription. Riffenberg and Trier do not appear to be named in this cipher note.

Fraction after the crib pass: about **78%** secure or probable, counting P- as probable.

Still unread: l.3 `‡‡ ~`, l.4 `d z xy 9`, l.5 `W xy u ʒ̊ d ℌ`, l.7 `z ss`, l.8-9 `ll ℌ z 9 tt`, l.11/17 `πꝫ` (on?),
