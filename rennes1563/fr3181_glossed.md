**The d-family.** d = e. La = d. ɑS/ɗ/uß = u/v. ∂ = m. ᑲ = e. The only s value in the family is the marked ᑲ̄ (= se). The f.55 runs where ᑲ seems to stand for s (l.9 "ᑲ ᑲ ɑS ᑲ", l.11 "sessions") get no support from these glosses.

## Colbert 390 (btv1b10033942k), checked for the f.55 residual signs
I surveyed views 121–125, 129–130, 133–134, 147–148, 150, 153, 157 and 159 at 1000 px, and zoomed p. 241 (view 121) at native resolution. Pages 221–231 were left to another agent.
- **These pages are in the f.52 hand, not the f.55/57 hand.** They use Y, λ, ʈ and ɣ, with the looped G. The ᑲ loop, the ɑS/ɗ sign and the ‡̲ of f.55/57 are rare or absent. Of the signs still unread in f.55, only **h** occurs in a clearly comparable form.
- **h = b**, glossed on p. 241 (margin "…mon beau filz"; cipher "ɱ h ℮ ʃ ʈ" = mon b e a u). This agrees with Tomokiyo's key (h = b).
- **No comparable instance found:** ꝥ (the crossed ℒ), ɼ, εʃ, ɾ, Ə, and the l.4/l.8/l.9 runs. Tomokiyo's list of nulls includes "eps3" and "r-hook", which may be f.55's εʃ and ɾ. That comes from the key reconstruction, not from a gloss.
- **Not done:** a full sign-by-sign alignment of these pages. The margins are dense paraphrases, and the pages are in the other hand, so an alignment would transfer little to f.55.
